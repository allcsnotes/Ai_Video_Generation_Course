# M07-L01 · Getting Started with Flow

> Video: Module 7, Lesson 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

So far every clip in this course came from Kling. Now you add a second tool: **Google Flow**, with a video model called **Omni**. Omni makes the picture and the sound together, from one prompt.

## What you'll learn

- Which Google plan gives you Flow, what it costs in India and how many credits it gives
- A tour of a Flow project: All media, Characters, Scenes, the prompt box, Agent and the settings button
- The settings: Video, the shape, the model (Omni 1.2 Flash), quality, length and how many videos at once
- How to read the cost before you click, and why the default costs 48 credits
- How to make and download your first 8 second test at 360p for 8 credits
- A simple credit plan: test cheap at 360p, fix the prompt, then make the final at 720p

## Before you start

- **Account:** a Google account with a Google AI plan. The cheapest plan with Flow is **Google AI Plus**. In India the video shows **₹399 a month**, with **200 Flow credits every month**. Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.
- **Credits** are what you spend to make videos. Your account menu shows how many are left and the date they refill.
- **What spends credits in this lesson:** only step 8 (the first test, **8 credits**). Opening Flow, making a project and changing settings are free.
- **Watermark:** in India, Flow adds a small visible watermark to every video, and you cannot turn it off.

## Follow along

### 1 · Pick the plan

The cheapest Google AI plan with Flow is **Google AI Plus**: ₹399 a month in India, 200 Flow credits every month. Your account menu shows **what is left** and **when it refills**.

### 2 · Open Flow

Go to `flow.google.com` with the same Google account.

What you should see: a **PLUS** badge at the top.

### 3 · Make a project

Click **New project** and give it a name. The video calls it **Chai Corner**.

What you should see, on the left:

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| All media | Holds everything you make |
| Characters | Helps you keep people the same |
| Scenes | Helps you keep places the same |

At the bottom is the **prompt box**, where you type what you want to see and hear.

### 4 · Turn Agent off and open the settings

Turn **Agent** off for now, so you choose every setting yourself. Then click the small settings button on the right of the prompt box.

### 5 · Video and the shape

Choose **Video**, then the shape:

- **16:9** for YouTube
- **9:16** for Reels and Shorts

### 6 · The model

Open the model list and pick **Omni 1.2 Flash**: the newest, and the default. This is the one the course uses.

- Some models say **Leaving December 5**. Skip those, they are going away.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite** stays. It is cheaper, but it has fewer options.

Below the model you set three more things:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Quality | 360p or 720p |
| Length | 3 to 15 seconds |
| How many | how many videos to make at once (x1, x2 and so on) |

### 7 · Read the cost before you click

At the bottom of the settings, Flow shows the cost before you generate.

| Settings | Cost |
|---|---|
| Default (720p, 8 s, x2) | 48 credits |
| 720p, 8 s, **x1** | 24 credits |
| **360p**, 8 s, x1 | 8 credits |

Why 48? The default makes **two videos at once**. Set it to one and the cost halves. Then switch from 720p to 360p and it drops to 8.

The rule: **720p costs 3 credits a second, 360p costs 1 credit a second.** 360p looks softer, but it is perfect for a test: you see the motion and hear the sound, for a third of the price.

### 8 · Your first test (spends 8 credits)

Type the prompt (below): a chai stall at sunrise, a kettle pouring chai, and the sounds you want to hear.

Settings: **9:16 · 360p · 8 s · x1** = 8 credits. Click the **arrow**.

What you should see: in about half a minute the clip is ready. The kettle pours, the steam rises, and you can hear the chai and the birds. The sound came from the same prompt.

Look at the corner: the small watermark Flow adds to every video in India.

### 9 · Download the clip

Click the **three dots** on the clip > **Download** > **360p** (original size).

## Prompts used

The first test (9:16, 360p, 8 s, x1):

```text
A small roadside chai stall in India at sunrise. A brass kettle pours hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter. Steam rises in the soft golden light. Sounds: the chai pouring, the kettle bubbling, birds far away. No people. No text.
```

## The plan for every clip

`Test at 360p (8 credits) > fix the prompt > final at 720p (24 credits)`

What 200 credits buy (8 second clips):

| Clip | Cost | How many from 200 |
|---|---|---|
| Test · 360p, 8 s, x1 | 8 | 25 tests |
| Final · 720p, 8 s, x1 | 24 | about 8 final clips |

## Common mistakes

1. **Leaving it on two videos at once.** x2 is the default, and it doubles every cost. Set x1.
2. **Picking a model that is leaving.** Skip every model marked Leaving.
3. **Testing at 720p.** 360p shows the same mistakes, for a third of the price.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: make one 8 second test at 360p, and listen to the sound. Did you get what you wrote?
2. Before you click, read the cost line. Change x1 to x2 and 360p to 720p and write down each cost.
3. Work out how many 5 second tests at 360p fit in 200 credits.

## Answers

1. Good looks like: Video, 9:16, Omni 1.2 Flash, 360p, 8 s, x1, and the cost line says 8 credits. The clip plays the sounds you named.
2. 360p x1 = 8, 360p x2 = 16, 720p x1 = 24, 720p x2 = 48.
3. A 5 second 360p clip costs 5 credits (1 a second), so 200 ÷ 5 = 40 tests.
