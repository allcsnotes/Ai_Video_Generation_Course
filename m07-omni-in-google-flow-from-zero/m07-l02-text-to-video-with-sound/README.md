# M07-L02 · Text to Video with Sound

> Video: Module 7, Lesson 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

In the last lesson one prompt gave you a clip with sound, but you did not choose that sound. Today you choose it: a voice, the background and the small sounds, all written in the prompt.

## What you'll learn

- The three layers of sound in a clip: background, effects and voice (and music as a fourth thing)
- What happens when a prompt has no sound words at all
- The sound formula: the picture first, then Background, Effects, Voice, then No music
- How changing only the background changes the whole mood
- Four rules that make sound prompts work every time

## Before you start

- Flow with Omni 1.2 Flash, set up as in lesson 1 (Agent off).
- Every test in this lesson uses the same settings: **9:16 · 360p · 8 s · x1 = 8 credits**. The video makes three tests, so **24 credits** in all.

## Key ideas

### Three layers of sound

| Layer | What it is | Examples from the video |
|---|---|---|
| Background | The place itself | a quiet street, birds, a busy market |
| Effects | The small sounds of things that happen | the chai pouring, a glass on the wood |
| Voice | Someone speaking, on screen or off screen | a warm woman's voice |
| Music | A fourth thing | if you do not want it, write: no music |

### Test 1: no sound words

The first test describes only the picture: the stall, the kettle, the steam. 360p, 8 seconds, one video, 8 credits.

Result: the picture is good, but the sound is **very faint**. The model had to guess what you wanted. If you do not ask for a sound, you cannot count on getting it.

### Test 2: the sound written in

The same scene, with three short parts added after the picture:

| Part | Text |
|---|---|
| Picture | the same picture as test 1 |
| Background | a quiet street waking up, birds far away. |
| Effects | the chai pouring, then the glass tapping on the wood. |
| Voice | a warm woman's voice, off screen, says softly: "Good morning. Fresh chai is ready." |
| End | No music. |

Same settings, 8 credits. Result: you hear the street, the pour, the voice and the tap of the glass. Every sound you asked for is there, and it is much louder and clearer than the first test. The tap even comes right when the glass touches the wood: Omni matches the sound to the picture.

### Test 3: change the place, change the mood

Keep the pour and the effects, but move the stall to a busy evening market. The background becomes many people chatting and scooter horns far away. No voice this time.

Same kettle, same pour. But the background turns a calm morning into a busy evening.

| | Test 2 | Test 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Mood | Calm morning | Busy evening |
| Background | quiet street, birds | people chatting, scooter horns |
| Voice | a soft voice | none |

### Four rules for sound

1. **Exact words, in quotes, and short.** About ten words fit in 8 seconds: `"Good morning. Fresh chai is ready."`
2. **Say who speaks, and how.** A warm woman's voice, softly. Write **off screen** if you do not want a face.
3. **Two or three sounds, in order.** Use the word **then** to put them in order: the chai pouring, then the glass tapping.
4. **No music**, unless you really want music.

## Worked example

The three prompts from the video, all at 9:16, 360p, 8 s, x1 (8 credits each).

Test 1, no sound words:

```text
A small roadside chai stall in India at sunrise. A brass kettle pours hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter. Steam rises in the soft golden light. No people. No text.
```

Test 2, sound written in:

```text
A small roadside chai stall in India at sunrise. A brass kettle pours hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter. Steam rises in the soft golden light. No people. No text. Background: a quiet street waking up, birds far away. Effects: the chai pouring, then the glass tapping on the wood. Voice: a warm woman's voice, off screen, says softly: "Good morning. Fresh chai is ready." No music.
```

Test 3, a new place:

```text
A small roadside chai stall in India in the busy evening. A brass kettle pours hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter. Steam rises in the warm orange street light. No people in the shot. No text. Background: a busy evening market, many people chatting, scooter horns far away. Effects: the chai pouring, then the glass tapping on the wood. No music.
```

What came out:

- Test 1: a good picture, very faint sound.
- Test 2: street, pour, voice and glass tap, much louder; the tap lands on the picture.
- Test 3: the same pour, but the crowd fills every second: a new mood.

## Common mistakes

- **No sound words.** The model guesses, and the sound may be faint or wrong.
- **A long line.** More than about ten words will not fit in 8 seconds.
- **Not saying who speaks.** Without "off screen", the model may show a face you did not want.
- **Too many sounds.** Keep it to two or three, in order with "then".
- **Forgetting "No music".** The model may add music you did not ask for.

## Try it yourself

2. Keep your test 2 prompt and change only the Background line to a new place. Listen to how the mood changes.
3. Write a voice line for your own stall in 10 words or fewer.
