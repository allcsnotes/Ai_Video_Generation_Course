# M07-L05 · Flow Basics

> Video: Module 7, Lesson 5. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

One Omni clip is only 8 seconds, but an ad often needs 15 or 20. Flow can make a shot longer, join clips into a scene, and keep your project tidy.

## What you'll learn

- How to open a clip in the Flow editor, and what the timeline shows
- How to **Extend** a shot by 4 seconds with a new line (1 credit for each second)
- How to **Add clip** to join two clips into a **scene**, and give it a name
- How to make a **collection** in the Library and drag videos into it
- What the stack icon means, and four tips for Flow

## Before you start

- Flow with Omni 1.2 Flash, and at least two clips in your project. The video uses the Meera talking clip from lesson 4 (8 seconds) and the kettle shot.
- **What spends credits:** only Extend. **4 seconds = 4 credits** (one credit for each second). Opening the editor, Add clip, scenes, renaming and collections are free.

## Follow along

### 1 · Open the editor

Click any video in your project. Flow opens it in the **editor**.

What you should see: at the top, the video. At the bottom, a **timeline**, with one mark for each second.

### 2 · The plus button

Next to the clip there is a **+** button. It gives two choices: **Add clip** and **Extend**.

### 3 · Extend

Click **Extend**.

What you should see: a grey block appears after the clip. That is the new part.

### 4 · Set the length (spends credits)

Click the length button and pick **4 s**. 4 seconds costs **4 credits**: one credit for each second.

### 5 · Write only what happens next

The look carries on by itself, so write only what happens next: she lowers the glass, waves, and says "See you tomorrow!" (full prompt below).

The bar shows **4s · Omni 1.2 Flash · Extend**.

### 6 · Generate

Click the **arrow** and wait. Flow shows **Extending** while it works.

What you should see: about a minute later, the clip is **12 seconds** long (the first 8 seconds plus 4 new ones).

### 7 · Check the join

Play the end of the clip and watch where the old part stops.

What you should see: no cut. The same Meera, the same light. The new part carries on from the last frame, and she says the new line in the same voice.

### 8 · Add clip: build a scene

Click **+** > **Add clip**. Flow shows the videos in this project. Pick the **kettle shot** and click **Add media**.

What you should see: Flow makes a **scene**: the Meera clip, then the kettle clip, on one timeline.

### 9 · Name the scene

Click the name at the top and give the scene a real name: **Meera welcome ad**.

Every scene also shows up in **Scenes**, on the left.

### 10 · Make a collection

Next to **Library**, click **+** to make a collection. The video calls it **Meera clips**.

### 11 · Drag videos in

Drag a video onto the collection. The video is now inside.

See the **stack icon** on a video? It means the video has more than one version: here, the first clip and the extended one.

Give every scene and collection a clear name, so you can find it next week.

## Prompts used

The extend prompt (Extend, 4 s, Omni 1.2 Flash):

```text
She lowers the glass, waves at the camera and says happily: "See you tomorrow!" The camera stays still. No music. No subtitles.
```

## Four tips for Flow

| # | Tip | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small steps | extend 4 s, check, extend again | check each step before the next |
| 2 | Only what happens next | She waves and says: "..." | Flow keeps the face and the light |
| 3 | A scene does not hide jumps | plan your shots first | plan the shot list in Claude first |
| 4 | One collection each | one ad or one character | one place for each ad |

## Common mistakes

1. **Extending in one big step.** Go 4 seconds at a time and check each one.
2. **Describing the look again in an extend prompt.** Write only what happens next; the look carries on.
3. **Expecting a scene to hide a jump.** A scene joins clips, but a jump between them still shows. Plan your shots first.
4. **Leaving default names.** Name every scene and collection clearly.

## Your task

Extend one of your clips by 4 seconds (4 credits), and give it a new line. Write only what happens next.
