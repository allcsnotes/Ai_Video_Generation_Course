# M11-L02 · Cutting and Arranging

> Video: Module 11, Lesson 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

The video first plays our three AI shots in the order they were made: a pour, a close-up, then a wide shot. Then the same three shots, cut and arranged into a story. This lesson shows how.

## What you'll learn

- How a short ad works as a tiny story in three beats
- How to reorder shots on CapCut's magnetic main track
- Why you check every AI clip frame by frame
- How to cut with the playhead, Split and Delete
- How to fit the shots and the music to the voiceover

## Before you start

- CapCut on the computer, with the project from the last lesson: three shots on the main track, the voiceover and the music on their own tracks.
- Nothing in this lesson spends credits.

### A tiny story in three beats

| Beat | Shot | What it tells |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The place | Wide shot | Where we are: a small chai stall |
| 2 · The action | The pour | What happens here |
| 3 · The payoff | Close-up | A smile, and a glass of chai |

So the wide shot goes **first**, not last.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Put the shots in story order

The timeline from the last lesson has the shots in the order they were made: pour, close-up, wide.

1. Click the **wide shot**.
2. Drag it to the very start.

What you should see: the other two slide along to make room. The main track is **magnetic**, so there are never any gaps between the shots.

### Step 2 · Check every clip frame by frame

AI clips often go wrong at the start or the end. So before you cut, watch every clip slowly, frame by frame.

The pour clip (`1_pour.mp4`, 5 seconds):

| Part | What happens | Keep? |
|---|---|---|
| Start, about the first 2 s | Slow camera move, nothing happens yet | Cut |
| Middle, about 2 s | The good part: the pour | Keep |
| Near the end | The tea stream vanishes in mid air, and steam covers the apron | Cut |

### Step 3 · Move the playhead to the start of the good part

Click the **ruler** (the time strip above the tracks), then use the **arrow keys** to move one frame at a time.

### Step 4 · Split

Select the clip, and click **Split**. Now it is two pieces.

### Step 5 · Delete the bad start

Click the left piece, and press **Delete**. The gap closes by itself.

### Step 6 · Cut the glitch at the end

Do the same at the glitch: move the playhead to it, **Split**, then delete the end piece.

You can also drag the **edge** of a clip to trim it. Split is just more exact.

### Step 7 · Fit it to the voice

Now the shots run about **12 seconds**, the same length as the voiceover:

| Shot | Length |
|---|---|
| Wide | 5.0 s |
| Pour | 1.9 s |
| Close-up | 5.0 s |

### Step 8 · Cut the music to the end

The music still runs on past the end.

1. Put the playhead on the last frame.
2. Select the music, click **Split**.
3. Delete the rest.

Short shots keep people watching. Most Reels change shot every **2 to 5 seconds**.

### Step 9 · Watch it all the way through

Press play, and watch it all the way through, with the sound on.

## Prompts used

No AI prompts in this lesson. The cutting routine:

```text
Click the ruler > arrow keys to the exact frame > select the clip > Split > select the bad piece > Delete
```

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: arrange three shots into a story, trim every bad frame, and cut the music to fit.
2. For each of your shots, write which beat it is: the place, the action, or the payoff.
3. Watch one of your AI clips frame by frame and write down the time where it goes wrong.

What good looks like: the place, then the action, then the payoff; no frozen camera moves or vanishing objects left in; the music ends where the last shot ends.

## Common mistakes

- **Keeping the order the shots were made in.** Arrange them as a story: place, action, payoff.
- **Watching clips at full speed only.** AI glitches hide in a few frames at the start or the end.
- **Trimming by dragging when you need an exact frame.** Split at the playhead is more exact.
- **Forgetting the music.** It runs past the end until you split and delete it.
- **Long shots.** Most Reels change shot every 2 to 5 seconds.
