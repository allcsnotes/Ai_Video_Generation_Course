# M04-L05 · Products and Text

> Video: Module 4, Lesson 5. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

A chai tin, a poster and a logo. All three were made in ChatGPT. Look at the words: Chai Corner. Masala Chai. ₹20. Every letter is right. Old AI tools wrote nonsense letters. Today, ChatGPT can write clean words, if you ask the right way.

- How to make a product shot, a poster and a logo in ChatGPT

- Why small words can wobble in a video, and what to do instead

## Before you start

- **Account:** ChatGPT at `chatgpt.com`. No credits are used.
- **Your brand:** the video uses the course's chai stall, **Chai Corner**.

### A product shot has five parts

| # | Part | In the video |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The product and how it looks | a round, mustard yellow tin with a dark brown lid |
| 2 | The words on it, inside quote marks | "CHAI CORNER", then "Masala Chai" |
| 3 | The surface and a few props | a wooden table, some cardamom and two cinnamon sticks |
| 4 | A plain background and soft light | so nothing pulls the eye away |
| 5 | The angle and some empty space | front view, with room above the tin for a headline later |

### Four rules for text

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Put the exact words inside quote marks. Then ChatGPT copies them, letter by letter | `"CHAI CORNER"` |
| 2 | Say where each line goes | at the top · in the middle · at the bottom |
| 3 | Say the size and the colour | big bold cream letters on a dark brown background |
| 4 | Keep it short | a name and a price read well; a long paragraph does not |

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Make the tin

1. Start a new chat and type `Create an image.`
2. Then the product, the words in quote marks, the table and the props.
3. Then the background, the light, the angle, and `vertical 9:16`. Press send.

```text
Create an image. A product shot of a masala chai tin. The tin is round and mustard-yellow, with a dark brown lid. On the front, in big bold dark brown letters: "CHAI CORNER". Below it, in smaller letters: "Masala Chai". The tin stands on a light wooden table, with a few cardamom pods and two cinnamon sticks beside it. Plain warm cream background. Soft studio light from the left, a gentle shadow. Front view, the tin in the centre, empty space above it. Photorealistic, vertical 9:16.
```

What you should see: Chai Corner and Masala Chai, both spelled right. It also drew a glass of chai and spices on the tin. We did not ask for that, but it fits. Always look for extras like this.

### Step 2 · Make a poster

Same chat, a new prompt. Each line has its place, its size and its colour:

```text
Create an image. A simple poster for the Chai Corner stall. At the top, in big bold cream letters: "CHAI CORNER". In the middle: a glass of hot masala chai with steam. At the bottom, in mustard-yellow letters: "Masala Chai ₹20". Dark brown background. Flat and clean, easy to read from far away. Vertical 9:16.
```

What you should see: the result follows the plan, line by line.

### Step 3 · Make a logo

Keep a logo simple: one flat icon, the name, and only two colours on plain white. A square shape works best for a logo.

```text
Create a simple logo for a chai stall called "Chai Corner". A flat icon of a small chai glass with steam, inside a circle. Below it, the words "CHAI CORNER" in bold letters. Only two colors: dark brown and mustard yellow, on a plain white background. Flat and clean, no tiny details. Square 1:1.
```

What you should see: a chai glass in a circle, and the name below.

### Step 4 · A hard test: the menu

Now a hard test: a menu with ten items and ten prices, in small letters.

```text
Create an image. A chalkboard menu for Chai Corner, in small handwritten letters: Masala Chai ₹20, Ginger Chai ₹25, Elaichi Chai ₹25, Kulhad Chai ₹30, Lemon Tea ₹20, Bun Maska ₹30, Samosa ₹15, Bread Pakora ₹20, Vada Pav ₹20, Poha ₹25. At the bottom: "Open 6 am to 11 pm, every day. No plastic, please bring your own cup." Vertical 9:16.
```

Check each line against your prompt:

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Masala Chai | ₹20 |
| Ginger Chai | ₹25 |
| Elaichi Chai | ₹25 |
| Kulhad Chai | ₹30 |
| Lemon Tea | ₹20 |
| Bun Maska | ₹30 |
| Samosa | ₹15 |
| Bread Pakora | ₹20 |
| Vada Pav | ₹20 |
| Poha | ₹25 |

What you should see: this time, every word and every price came out right. But that is not always true, so check each line, one by one.

### Step 5 · Fix one thing with an edit

If one thing is wrong, don't start again. Edit it. In the video, one price is changed:

```text
Change only one price: Masala Chai ₹20 becomes ₹22. Keep every other word, price, picture and color exactly the same.
```

What you should see: only that one price changed (₹20 to ₹22). The other nine prices, the words and the pictures stayed the same.

### A tip for video

When a video tool moves the picture, small words can wobble. For video, keep words big and few, or add them later in the edit.

## Prompts used

All five prompts from the video are in the steps above, in order: the tin, the poster, the logo, the menu and the one-price edit. The pattern for any text:

```text
At the [top / middle / bottom], in [size] [colour] letters: "[EXACT WORDS]".
```

## Common mistakes

1. **No quote marks.** Then the AI guesses the words, and may change them (Chai Corner may come out as "Chai Korner").
2. **Too much small text.** More words means more chances for a wrong letter.
3. **Not checking.** One wrong price on a poster can cost you money (you wrote ₹20, the image says ₹200).

The fix: quote marks, few words, and check every letter.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: make one product picture and one poster for your own brand, then check every word.
2. Write a product prompt with all five parts. Did it add any extras you did not ask for?
3. Make a logo for your brand: one flat icon, the name in quote marks, two colours, plain white, square 1:1.

## Recap

- A product shot has five parts: product, words, surface, background and angle.
- Put the words in quote marks, and say where they go, how big, and what colour.
- Check every letter and every price. Fix one thing with an edit.
