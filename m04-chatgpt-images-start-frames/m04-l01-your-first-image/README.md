# M04-L01 · Your First Image

> Video: Module 4, Lesson 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

The same chai man, made from the same prompt, in two shapes: a tall one for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and a wide one for a normal YouTube video. What happens if you put the wide image into a tall video? The sides get cut off. You lose the pot, the steam and half the stall. So you pick the shape before you make the image.

## What you'll learn

- Why you make an image first, when what you want is a video
- How to pick the shape (the aspect ratio): 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1
- How to make your first image in ChatGPT on the free plan
- How to make a wide version of the same image with Resize, without starting again
- The five checks to run before an image becomes a start frame

## Before you start

- **Account:** a free ChatGPT account at `chatgpt.com`. The video uses the free plan.
- **Cost:** nothing. The free ChatGPT plan can make a few images a day. If you hit the limit, wait for it to reset. Limits and plans change, so check the current limits on your own account.
- **What you need from earlier:** the image prompt for shot 3 of the chai Reel (the chai man pouring at sunrise) from Module 3.

### Why make an image at all?

Because the image becomes the **first frame** of your clip (the very first picture the video starts from). It locks three things:

| The image locks | So the video tool only has to add |
|---|---|
| the face | the pour |
| the place | the steam |
| the light | a slow push in |

Then the video tool (Kling or Veo) only adds the motion, and your video prompt is about motion only. That is the fix from the last lesson: start from an image.

### Pick the shape first

The **aspect ratio** is the width compared to the height.

| Shape | Looks | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | Tall | Reels, Shorts and stories |
| 16:9 | Wide | YouTube videos and TV screens |
| 1:1 | Square | A post in the feed |

Make the image in the same shape as your final video. Then nothing gets cropped later.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open ChatGPT

Go to `chatgpt.com`. The free plan is enough.

What you should see: a new chat with an empty message box.

### Step 2 · Start with "Create an image."

Type the words `Create an image.` and then paste the image prompt for shot 3 of the chai Reel.

It is the same man and the same words as before, but with no camera move and no sound. An image prompt is just one frozen moment.

### Step 3 · End with the shape, then press Enter

At the end of the prompt, write the shape: `vertical 9:16`. Then press **Enter**.

What you should see: after about half a minute, the chai man pouring at sunrise. The image is **941 × 1672** pixels. That is exactly 9:16.

### Step 4 · Make a wide version with Resize

You do not have to start again. Change the shape of this image:

1. Click the image to open it.
2. At the bottom there is a row of tools. Click **Resize**.
3. You get five shapes: **Square, Portrait, Story, Landscape** and **Widescreen**. Pick **Widescreen 16:9**.

What you should see: ChatGPT does not just cut the picture. It paints more of the stall on both sides, and keeps the same man. The wide image is **1672 × 941** pixels.

Click path: `Click the image > Resize > Widescreen 16:9`

### Step 5 · Save the image

To save an image, click the **download arrow** at the top.

### Step 6 · Check it before you use it

Before you use an image as a start frame, check five things:

1. **Right shape?** 9:16 for a Reel.
2. **Face clear?** Is the face clear and sharp?
3. **Hands right?** Look at the hands and count the fingers.
4. **No strange text?** Any strange text or a fake logo?
5. **Room for the motion?** Here the chai has space to pour. If the pot touched the edge, the motion would get cut.

The full checklist, with the rules from the next lesson added, is in [start-frame-checklist.md](../../downloads/start-frame-checklist.md).

## Prompts used

The image prompt for shot 3 of the chai Reel, as typed in the video:

```text
Create an image. A friendly Indian man in his mid-50s, short salt-and-pepper beard, thin black-rimmed glasses, cream cotton half-sleeve kurta, red-and-white checked cotton towel on his left shoulder. He holds a steel pot high above a small glass, ready to pour chai. Chai stall at sunrise. Medium close-up. Golden light through steam. Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

The parts of it:

| Part | In this prompt |
|---|---|
| Start | `Create an image.` |
| Who | the chai man description |
| One frozen moment | holds a steel pot high above a small glass, ready to pour |
| Place | chai stall at sunrise |
| Shot and light | medium close-up, golden light through steam |
| Style and shape | photorealistic, vertical 9:16, no text |

## Common mistakes

1. **Forgetting the shape.** With no ratio in the prompt, you often get a square, and you have to crop it. Write `vertical 9:16` or `wide 16:9`.
2. **Cropping a wide image into a tall video.** You lose the sides. Use **Resize** instead.
3. **Asking for a long sign or words in the image.** Letters often come out wrong. Add text later, in the edit.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: make one start frame for a shot from your own shot list, in 9:16. Then resize it to 16:9 and run the five checks.
2. Make the same prompt once without any shape at the end. What shape do you get?
3. Your shot will be posted as a feed post on Instagram. Which shape do you pick?

## Recap

- The image is the first frame. It locks the face, the place and the light.
- Pick the shape first: 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube.
- Write the shape in the prompt, use Resize for other shapes, and check the image before you use it.
