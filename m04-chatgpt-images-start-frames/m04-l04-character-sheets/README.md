### The problem: one prompt twice = two people

The video ran this prompt twice, word for word:

```text
Create an image. Meera: a 28-year-old Indian woman with shoulder-length wavy black hair, warm brown eyes, small gold hoop earrings, a white shirt and a mustard-yellow apron. She smiles at the camera. Plain light background. Medium shot, soft daylight. Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

The two results were close, but they were two different women.

### What is a character sheet?

A **character sheet** is one picture that shows one character many times: from the front, from the side, and with different faces.

| Row | Views |
|---|---|
| Angles (top row) | front · three-quarter · side |
| Expressions (bottom row) | big smile · surprised · thinking |

Every view is made together, so it is the same person in every view. Then, for every new shot, you point ChatGPT to the sheet and say: use this exact person.

### A good sheet prompt has four parts

| # | Part | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Describe her in full | age, hair, eyes, earrings, clothes |
| 2 | List the views | top row: angles · bottom row: faces |
| 3 | Plain white background | and the same face, hair and clothes in every view |
| 4 | Shape, and no words | wide 16:9, no text, no labels |

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open a new chat

Open a new chat in ChatGPT.

### Step 2 · Start the sheet prompt

Start with `Create a character sheet for one character.` Then paste Meera's full description.

### Step 3 · Add the views, the background and the shape

Add the six views, the plain background, and the shape: wide 16:9, with no labels. Press send.

```text
Create a character sheet for one character. Meera: a 28-year-old Indian woman with shoulder-length wavy black hair, warm brown eyes, small gold hoop earrings, a white shirt and a mustard-yellow apron. Show her six times on one plain white background. Top row: front view, three-quarter view, side view. Bottom row: a big smile, surprised, thinking. Same face, same hair and same clothes in every view. Soft, even studio light. Photorealistic, wide 16:9, no text, no labels.
```

What you should see: the sheet. Six views of Meera, side by side. Look closely: the front, the three-quarter view and the side view, then a smile, surprise and thinking. Same face, same earrings, same apron.

### Step 4 · Use the sheet for a new shot

In the same chat (the sheet is right above), ask for a new shot. Say "use this exact Meera from the sheet", then the new shot, then what to keep:

```text
Now use this exact Meera from the sheet. Show her behind a small chai counter, pouring chai from a steel kettle into a glass. Keep her face, hair, earrings and apron exactly the same as in the sheet. Warm morning light, plain softly blurred background. Medium shot, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

What you should see: same face, same hair, same earrings, same apron.

### Step 5 · One more shot, and check it

The video asks for one more shot: a close-up, laughing with a glass of chai, with the glass near her chin.

What you should see: still the same Meera. But she is holding the glass up to her lips, not near her chin. Small details can change, so always check each shot.

Check each new shot against the sheet:

| Same as the sheet? | Check |
|---|---|
| Face | yes / no |
| Hair | yes / no |
| Earrings | yes / no |
| Apron | yes / no |
| The detail you asked for | yes / no |

```text
Now use this exact Meera from the sheet. Show her behind a small chai counter, pouring chai from a steel kettle into a glass. Keep her face, hair, earrings and apron exactly the same as in the sheet. Warm morning light, plain softly blurred background. Medium shot, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

## Common mistakes

1. **Changing the description.** If you write curly hair in one prompt and wavy hair in the next, you get two people. Change one word, and you get a new person.
2. **A busy background.** Props and colours pull the eye away from the face. Use plain white.
3. **Too many tiny views.** Twelve small faces are hard to copy. Six big views work better.

The fix: same words every time, plain white, six big views.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: make a character sheet for your own character. Then make two new shots with them, and check that they look the same.
2. Write your character's full description once, and save it. Use it word for word in every prompt.
3. In your second shot, ask for one small detail (like where a hand goes). Did it come out exactly as asked?

## Recap

- One prompt twice gives two different people. A sheet gives you one.
- Full description, six views, plain white background, no labels.
- For every new shot, say: use this exact person from the sheet. Then check the face.
