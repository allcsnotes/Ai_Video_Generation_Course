# M04-L03 · Editing an Image

> Video: Module 4, Lesson 3. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Our chai man in warm morning light. Then on a rainy evening. Then in a yellow kurta. Then cut out, with no background at all. Did we write a new prompt for each one? No. We edited the same image. The face, the pose and the frame all stayed the same.

## What you'll learn

- Why you edit an image instead of starting again
- The two parts of every good edit: what to change and what to keep
- How to edit with words in ChatGPT: a light edit and a clothes edit
- How to use the Erase and Remove BG tools, and why you always check the result
- Three editing mistakes

## Before you start

- **Account:** ChatGPT at `chatgpt.com`, and an image you made there (the video uses the strong first frame from the last lesson).
- **Cost:** no credits. As in M04-L01, the free ChatGPT plan can make a few images a day; if you hit the limit, wait for it to reset.

### Why edit, and not just start again?

| | Start again | Edit |
|---|---|---|
| What you send | A new prompt | The same image, one change |
| What you get | A whole new picture: new face, new place, new frame | Same face, same pose, same frame |

In a video, your man must look the same in every shot. An edit keeps everything you like, and changes only what you ask for.

### Every good edit has two parts

1. **Say what to change.** `Change only his kurta to a mustard yellow kurta.`
2. **Say what to keep.** `Keep the same face, the same pose, the same light and the same frame.`

Change one thing at a time. Check the result, then make the next change.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open the image

Click your image to open it. It opens large.

### Step 2 · Find the tools and the edit box

At the bottom are the tools: **Markup, Comment, Remove BG, Erase** and **Resize**. Below them is a box to describe your edit.

### Step 3 · Edit the light

In the edit box, say what to change, then say what to keep. Press send.

```text
Change the light to a rainy blue evening. Wet counter, soft rain in the background, a warm lamp glow on his face. Keep the same man, the same pose and the same frame.
```

What you should see: same man, same pose. Now it is a rainy evening, with a lamp above him.

### Step 4 · Edit the clothes

Next, the clothes. Change only his kurta, and keep everything else:

```text
Change only his kurta to a mustard yellow cotton kurta. Keep the same face, the same towel, the same pose, the same light and the same frame.
```

What you should see: only his clothes changed. Everything else stayed the same.

### Step 5 · Erase: remove a thing with a brush

Some edits do not need words. They have their own tools.

1. Click the **Erase** tool.
2. Brush over what you want to remove. In the video: one small steel pot.
3. Press send.

What you should see: the pot is gone. But look closely: so are the other small pots, and the clay pot too. This tool can take more than you brushed. So always compare the before and the after.

Click path: `Click the image > Erase > brush over the thing > send`

### Step 6 · Remove BG: cut out the background

Click **Remove BG**. One click, and the man stands on nothing.

What you should see: where the background was, it is now see-through. Later, you can put him on any background you like.

Click path: `Click the image > Remove BG`

## Prompts used

The light edit:

```text
Change the light to a rainy blue evening. Wet counter, soft rain in the background, a warm lamp glow on his face. Keep the same man, the same pose and the same frame.
```

The clothes edit:

```text
Change only his kurta to a mustard yellow cotton kurta. Keep the same face, the same towel, the same pose, the same light and the same frame.
```

The edit pattern you can reuse:

```text
Change only [one thing] to [the new version]. Keep the same [face], the same [pose], the same [light] and the same [frame].
```

## Common mistakes

1. **Many changes in one edit.** New light, new clothes and a new place, all at once (for example: "Change the light to night, his kurta to blue, the place to a beach"). Too much changes, and the face can drift.
2. **Forgetting to say what to keep.** "Change his kurta to yellow" with no "keep" line: the AI may change the face or the frame too.
3. **Not checking.** Compare every edit with the original before you use it. Erase, for example, removed more pots than were brushed.

The fix: one change, say what to keep, and check every edit.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: take your first frame image. Make one light edit and one clothes edit in ChatGPT, then compare each one with the original.
2. Use Erase to remove one small thing. Did it take only what you brushed?
3. Rewrite this edit so it follows the rules: "Make it night, change his shirt to blue and put him on a beach."

## Recap

- Edit, don't start again. An edit keeps your man the same.
- Say what to change and what to keep. One change at a time.
- Erase and Remove BG are quick fixes. Always check the result.
