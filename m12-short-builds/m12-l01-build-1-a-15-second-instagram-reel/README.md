# M12-L01 · Build 1: A 15-Second Instagram Reel

> Video: Module 12, Build 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

In this module we stop learning single tools and build complete videos. The first build is a **15-second Instagram Reel** for Chai Corner, made with **Kling's free plan** and **Shotcut** (a free video editor).

## What you'll build

- A 15-second upright Reel (9:16) for Chai Corner: three shots of five seconds each
- One short caption on each shot, and soft music under all three (no voice)
- A finished file: 1080 × 1920, 30 frames per second, 15 seconds long

## Before you start

- **Kling** account (free plan is enough). Module 5 shows Kling from zero.
- **Shotcut** installed (free).
- One music track you are allowed to use (Module 10 covers free, copyright-safe music).
- **Credits:** on Kling's free plan a 5-second clip at 720p costs **30 credits**, so three shots cost **90 credits**. Every Generate click spends credits, and so does every remake. Check your balance first. Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.
- Plan it with the [build plan template](../../downloads/build-plan-template.md).

## Plan

**The idea, in one line:** a rainy evening, and a hot glass of chai waiting at Chai Corner.

| Shot | Time | What we see | Caption | Tool · cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 5 s | Wide: the stall in the rain | Rainy evening? | Kling · 30 credits |
| 2 | 5 to 10 s | Close-up: chai being poured | Hot ginger chai | Kling · 30 credits |
| 3 | 10 to 15 s | Meera holds out a glass | Chai Corner / open till 10 pm | Kling · 30 credits |
| | | | **Total** | **90 credits** |

- Fifteen seconds fits three shots of five seconds each.
- One short **caption** per shot, soft **music** under all three, **no voice**.

## Build it step by step

### Step 1 · Generate the shots in Kling

1. Open Kling. Pick **Video Generation**, **Kling 4.0 Flash**, **720p**, **5 s** and **9:16**.
2. Type the first prompt. It has five parts: the **shot size**, the **place**, the **light**, **what moves**, and the **camera**. Then click **Generate** (the button shows 30, the credits it will spend).
3. Kling makes **one clip at a time**, in about a minute (the card says "Creating... Estimated wait: 1 minute"). When it is done, type the next prompt: a close-up of chai, poured from a brass kettle.
4. For the last shot, ask for Meera in a mustard apron, holding a glass out towards the camera.

### Step 2 · Check every clip, then download

1. Watch every clip, from start to end. All three are clean in the video.
2. If one has a glitch, **change the prompt and make that shot again** (another 30 credits).
3. Download each clip, and number the names in order: `1_stall.mp4`, `2_pour.mp4`, `3_offer.mp4`.

### Step 3 · Edit in Shotcut

1. **New project:** give it a name, and set **Video mode** to **Vertical HD 30 fps**.
2. **Add the clips:** open each clip, and press **A**. It goes to the end of the timeline. Three clips, fifteen seconds, in order.
3. **Music:** add an audio track, and put the music on it. Set the music to end at 15 seconds, and **mute the sound of the clips** (the mute icon on the V1 track).
4. **Fade:** select the music, open **Filters**, and add **Fade Out Audio**. Make it **2 seconds** long, so the music ends softly.
5. **Caption 1:** select clip one, and add the **Text: Simple** filter. Type the caption, then set its position and size, so it sits in the lower middle of the frame. The video used Position **90, 1400**, Size **900 × 200**, Horizontal fit Center, Vertical fit Middle.
6. **Captions 2 and 3:** do the same for the other two clips. The last caption names the stall and says when it is open: `Chai Corner` / `open till 10 pm`.

Tip: in Shotcut, click the filter search box before you type. If the box is not focused, your letters work as timeline shortcuts.

### Step 4 · Export

1. Click **Export**, pick the **YouTube** preset, and click **Export Video/Audio**. Name the file and save.
2. Open the **Jobs** panel. The job gets a **green tick** when it is done.

## Prompts used

Shot 1 (wide):

```text
Wide shot of a small roadside chai stall at dusk in light rain. A yellow sign above it reads CHAI CORNER. Warm bulbs glow under a green tarp, and a brass kettle sits on the stove. Raindrops fall past the warm light. The camera slowly pushes in. Cosy, cinematic, photo-real.
```

Shot 2 (the pour):

```text
Close-up of hot ginger chai being poured from a brass kettle into a small glass on a wooden counter at a roadside chai stall. Warm golden bulb light. Soft rain falls in the blurred background. Slow motion, shallow depth of field, cinematic, photo-real.
```

Shot 3 (Meera offers a glass):

```text
Medium shot of a smiling young Indian woman in a mustard apron at her roadside chai stall at dusk. She holds out a small glass of chai towards the camera, as if offering it to the viewer. Warm bulb light behind her, soft rain in the background. The camera holds still. One continuous shot. Cinematic, photo-real.
```

Settings for all three: `Video Generation · Kling 4.0 Flash · 720p · 5 s · 9:16` (30 credits each).

## Check your work

- [ ] The file is **1080 × 1920**, **30 frames per second**, and **15 seconds** long
- [ ] Three clips in order: stall, pour, offer
- [ ] The clips' own sound is muted; only the music plays
- [ ] The music ends softly (2-second Fade Out Audio)
- [ ] Each shot has one short caption in the lower middle, easy to read
- [ ] You watched it on your phone
- [ ] When you post it, you mark it as made with AI (Module 14 shows you how)

## Common mistakes

- **Not watching every clip to the end.** A glitch near the end is easy to miss. Remake that one shot only.
- **Clips out of order.** Number the file names (`1_`, `2_`, `3_`) before you open them in Shotcut.
- **Forgetting to mute the clips.** Their own sound plays under your music.
- **Music that stops dead.** Add Fade Out Audio, 2 seconds.
- **Long captions.** One short line per shot; the viewer has only five seconds to read it.
- **Typing a caption while the filter search is not focused.** Shotcut treats the letters as shortcuts.

## Your task

Make your own 15-second Reel for a small shop you know: one idea, three shots of five seconds, one caption each, soft music.

What good looks like: a one-line idea, three prompts with all five parts (shot size, place, light, what moves, camera), 90 credits spent (plus any remakes), and a 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 15-second file that you checked on your phone.

## Recap

1. One idea, three shots, five seconds each.
2. One prompt per shot in Kling, and check every clip.
3. In Shotcut: clips in order, music with a fade, and a caption on each shot.
4. Export, check the file, and watch it on your phone.
