# M12-L02 · Build 2: A Product Ad with Voice and Music

> Video: Module 12, Build 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Build 2 is a **16-second product ad** with a real voice and music, made with **Kling**, **Google Flow** and **Shotcut**. One product, one spoken line, three shots.

## What you'll build

- A 16-second upright ad (9:16) for the Chai Corner masala chai tin
- Shot 1: the tin, made in Kling from a photo of the tin
- Shot 2: the chai pour, reused from Build 1
- Shot 3: Meera holds the tin and speaks one line, made in Flow with her voice and lip sync
- Music under it all, turned down so the voice stays clear, and a short caption on each shot

## Before you start

- **Kling** account (Module 5) and a **photo of your product** (here, the Chai Corner tin).
- **Google Flow** with a plan that has credits (Module 7), and the **Meera character** saved in Flow (Module 9).
- The pour clip from [Build 1](../m12-l01-build-1-a-15-second-instagram-reel/), and a music track you are allowed to use (Module 10).
- **Shotcut** installed (free).
- **Credits:** the tin clip costs **30 Kling credits**. The pour is **free**, because we reuse it. The Meera clip costs **18 Flow credits** (720p, 6 s). Remakes cost the same again. Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.
- Plan it with the [build plan template](../../downloads/build-plan-template.md).

## Plan

**The idea, in one line:** Chai Corner now sells its masala chai in a tin, so you can make it at home.

| Shot | What we see | Tool | Cost | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The tin, from a photo | Kling (Omni reference) | 30 Kling credits | New: Masala Chai |
| 2 | The chai pour | reused from Build 1 | free | Real ginger, real spice |
| 3 | Meera speaks | Flow (Omni, Ingredients) | 18 Flow credits | Order at Chai Corner |

**Meera's line** is short, so it fits in six seconds:

```text
Chai Corner masala chai. Now make our chai at home!
```

Reuse good clips: a reused clip costs nothing.

## Build it step by step

### Step 1 · Shot 1: the tin, in Kling

1. Open Kling, and pick **Omni reference**. Click the **+**, then **Upload**, and add the photo of the tin.
2. In the prompt, type **@** and pick **Image1**, so the prompt points to the photo. Ask for a slow push in, on a warm counter, in morning light.
3. Add one important line: **keep the tin, its lid and its label exactly like the picture.** Click **Generate** (30 credits).
4. When it is done, **check the label, letter by letter.** In the video, CHAI CORNER and Masala Chai are both correct, and the lid and colours match the photo. If the text is wrong, make it again.

### Step 2 · Shot 2: reuse the pour

Use the pour clip from Build 1. It costs nothing.

### Step 3 · Shot 3: Meera speaks, in Flow

Meera must speak, so we use Flow (Omni makes the voice and the lip movement together).

1. Pick **Video**, **Ingredients**, **9:16**, **Omni**, **720p**, and **6 s**. The count is x1. It uses **18 credits**.
2. Click **+**, and add two ingredients: the **tin photo**, and the **Meera character**.
3. In the prompt, write the spoken line **inside quote marks, after the word says**. Then click the **arrow** to generate.
4. Flow gives Meera the voice and moves her lips with the words. **Listen to every word** before you use it. Voice, words and lip movement must all match.

### Step 4 · Edit in Shotcut

1. New project called **Chai Corner ad**, in **Vertical HD 30 fps**. Open the three clips, and press **A** for each one.
2. Clips 1 and 2 have their own sound, and we do not want it. Select clip 1, open **Filters**, click **+**, and add **Mute**. Do the same for clip 2.
3. **Do not mute clip 3.** That is Meera's voice.
4. Add an audio track, and put the music on it, ending at **16 seconds**. Add **Gain / Volume** to the music and set it to **-12 dB**, so the voice stays clear.
5. Add **Fade Out Audio** to the music too, **2 seconds** long, so it ends softly.
6. Last, add **Text: Simple** to each clip, with a short caption. Short captions stay big and easy to read.

### Step 5 · Export

1. Click **Export**, pick the **YouTube** preset, and click **Export Video/Audio**. Save.
2. Wait for the **green tick** in the Jobs panel.

## Check your work

- [ ] The tin's label is correct, letter by letter (CHAI CORNER, Masala Chai), and the lid and colours match the photo
- [ ] Meera says every word of the line, and her lips match
- [ ] Clips 1 and 2 are muted; clip 3 is not
- [ ] Music ends at 16 s, at -12 dB, with a 2-second fade out
- [ ] One short caption on each clip
- [ ] The file is **1080 × 1920**, **30 fps**, **16 seconds**
- [ ] On your phone, the voice is clear over the music
- [ ] When you post it, you mark it as made with AI

## Common mistakes

- **Not pointing the prompt at the photo.** Type @ and pick Image1, or Kling may invent its own tin.
- **Not checking the label.** AI often changes letters on a product. Read it letter by letter, and remake if it is wrong.
- **A long line.** It must fit in six seconds. Keep it to one or two short sentences.
- **The line not in quote marks.** Put the spoken words in quote marks, after the word says.
- **Muting the wrong clip.** Mute clips 1 and 2, never the clip with the voice.
- **Music too loud.** Turn it down (-12 dB here) so every word is clear.

## Your task

Make a 16-second ad for one product, with one spoken line.

What good looks like: a one-line idea, a product shot made from a real photo with the label checked, one reused or cheap shot, one speaking shot with a short line in quote marks, music turned down under the voice, and a file you listened to on your phone.

## Recap

1. One idea, three shots, and one short spoken line.
2. Kling with a photo for the product, and keep the label exact.
3. Flow for the voice: ingredients, and the line in quote marks.
4. In Shotcut: mute the other clips, keep the voice, and turn the music down.
