# M12-L04 · Build 4: A Talking Character with Lip Sync

> Video: Module 12, Build 4. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Build 4 is a **talking character**: Meera explains a chai tip to the camera, and her lips move with her words. One picture, one voice, and Kling does the **lip sync** (moving the lips to match the voice).

## What you'll build

- An 18-second upright clip of Meera speaking a chai tip, with her own voice inside
- A free AI voice from **Google AI Studio**
- Lip sync from **Kling Avatar**, checked for lips, face, hands and eyes
- Captions in Shotcut, the same way as Build 3

## Before you start

A talking character needs three things: **a picture** of the character, **a voice**, and **a lip sync tool** that joins them.

- A picture of Meera (Modules 4 and 9 show how to make and keep a character).
- **Google AI Studio** (free) for the voice.
- **Kling** with enough credits for **Avatar**.
- **Shotcut** for captions (see [Build 3](../m12-l03-build-3-a-faceless-youtube-short/)).
- **Credits:** the voice is **free**. The lip sync costs **72 Kling credits** for 18 seconds, **about 4 credits for each second**, so a longer tip costs more. Each new try costs credits again. Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.
- Plan it with the [build plan template](../../downloads/build-plan-template.md).

## Build it step by step

### Step 1 · Her voice, in AI Studio

1. In AI Studio (`aistudio.google.com`), open **Generate speech**, and **create a new dialog**. Then click the **speaker**, to pick a voice.
2. Filter the list to **Feminine**, and pick a voice that fits her. The video chose **Cleo**: warm and engaging.
3. Type her tip into the speech block. In **Style**, write how she speaks: warm and friendly, like a chai seller sharing a kitchen secret.
4. Click **Run**, listen to every word, and download the **WAV** file. A wrong word? Change the text and Run again.

Her voice starts: "Here is my secret for strong masala chai."

### Step 2 · Lip sync, in Kling Avatar

1. In Kling, open the **type menu**, and choose **Avatar**.
2. Click **Character**, and upload her picture. Then click **Speech**, pick **Upload Audio**, and add the voice file.
3. In **Character Performance**, say how she moves: small natural nods, a friendly smile, the glass held still, and a still camera.
4. The **Generate** button shows the price: **72 credits**. Click it, and wait a few minutes.

### Step 3 · Check four things

Watch it with the sound on.

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| 1 · Lips | They must close on sounds like **M** and **B**. Look at "masala" and "milk". |
| 2 · Face | It must still look like Meera, from the first frame to the last. |
| 3 · Hands | The glass should stay still, and not bend or melt. |
| 4 · Eyes | They should blink and move naturally, not stare. |

The video's clip passes all four. Her free hand moves a little at the start, but it looks natural, so we keep it.

**If a check fails:** change the picture (calmer, facing the camera) or the performance words, and generate again. Each try costs credits, so choose the picture with care.

### Step 4 · Finish and post

1. Download the clip. It is **720 × 1280**, upright, **18 seconds** long, with her voice inside.
2. Add captions in Shotcut, the same way as Build 3, because many people watch with the sound off.
3. When you post it, answer **yes** to altered content. A real-looking person who speaks must be marked as made with AI.
4. **Never make a real person say words they did not say.**

## Prompts used

The tip (typed into AI Studio's speech block):

```text
Here is my secret for strong masala chai. Boil the water with the spices first, for two minutes. Then add the tea leaves, and only then the milk. That way, every spice gets time to open up. Try it at home, and tell me!
```

Voice style (AI Studio, Style; voice Cleo):

```text
Warm and friendly, like a chai seller sharing a kitchen secret with a regular customer. Gentle Indian English accent, natural pace, a smile in the voice.
```

Kling Avatar, Character Performance (with the picture as Character and the WAV as Speech):

```text
She talks warmly to the camera, with small natural nods and a friendly smile. She holds the glass of chai still near her chest. The camera stays still.
```

Settings: `Kling > type menu > Avatar · 720p` (72 credits for 18 seconds).

## Check your work

- [ ] The picture faces the camera, mouth closed, hands still
- [ ] Lips close on M and B ("masala", "milk")
- [ ] The face is still Meera from the first frame to the last
- [ ] The glass stays still and keeps its shape
- [ ] The eyes blink and move naturally
- [ ] The clip is 720 × 1280, upright, 18 seconds, with her voice inside

## Common mistakes

- **A busy picture.** A picture in the middle of a pour keeps moving while she talks. Pick a calm one.
- **A long tip.** Lip sync costs about 4 credits a second. Keep it short.
- **Not checking M and B sounds.** If the lips do not close, the lip sync looks fake.
- **Generating again and again without changing anything.** Change the picture or the performance words first.
- **Skipping the AI label.** A real-looking person who speaks must be marked as made with AI.
- **Using a real person's face or voice.** Never make a real person say words they did not say.

## Your task

Make your own character speak a 15-second tip: a calm picture, a free voice, then check all four.

What good looks like: a picture facing the camera with the mouth closed, a tip in simple spoken sentences, a free voice you listened to in full, about 60 credits of lip sync for 15 seconds at about 4 a second, and a clip that passes lips, face, hands and eyes.

## Recap

1. A talking character is one picture, one voice, and lip sync.
2. Pick a calm picture: facing the camera, mouth closed, hands still.
3. Make the voice free in AI Studio, then upload it to Kling Avatar.
4. Check the lips, the face, the hands, and the eyes, before you post.
