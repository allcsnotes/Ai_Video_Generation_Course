# M08-L01 · Kling vs Omni

> Video: Module 8, Lesson 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Kling or Omni? Both make great videos. So which one should you use? The video runs a fair test: the same prompts in both tools, **Kling 4.0 Flash** on kling.ai and **Omni 1.2 Flash** in Google Flow.

## What you'll learn

- How to run a fair test between two video tools
- Five things to compare: quality, motion, faces, text and sound
- What each tool did well and badly on the same prompts
- Why you must always write the sound you want, in both tools
- Why there is no single winner

## Key ideas

### The rules of a fair test

- **The same words** in both prompts.
- **The same start picture** (when the test uses one).
- **The same length**: 5 seconds.

The Kling clips are the ones made in Module 5. In Flow, the same prompt is pasted in. Test 1 runs at **720p** in Flow, like Kling (15 credits). The tests with Meera run at **360p** in Flow, to save credits (5 credits each).

| # | Test | What you look at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality | the pour, the steam, the light |
| 2 | Motion | a fast spin and zoom |
| 3 | Faces | does she stay the same? |
| 4 | Text | is the sign spelled right? |
| 5 | Sound | what do we hear? |

### Test 1: quality

A kettle pours hot chai into a glass. Watch the pour, the steam and the light.

- Both look great.
- **Kling** looks a little more like a real photo.
- **Omni's** steam curls look a little drawn, like a painting.

Winner: Kling, by a little.

### Test 2: motion

A wild prompt: the camera spins around her, then zooms into her face. Both tools start from the same picture.

- Both did the spin and the zoom.
- In the close-up, her face changes a little in both. Fast moves are hard for every tool.

Result: a tie.

### Test 3: faces

Start from the picture of Meera pouring chai. The prompt: she keeps pouring chai, looks up and smiles. Keep her face and clothes the same.

- Both keep her face.
- In **Kling**, the stream drifts away from the glass.
- **Omni** keeps the pour in the glass, right to the end.

Winner: Omni, by a little.

### Test 4: text

A woman holds a chalkboard sign that says Chai Corner, open 7 AM. Video tools often get letters wrong.

- Both spelled every word right: CHAI, CORNER, OPEN, 7 AM.
- Omni even drew a little cup on the sign.

Result: a tie.

### Test 5: sound

Both tools now make sound by themselves.

- **Kling faces clip:** she talks, but not in any real language. Nobody asked for a voice.
- **Omni sign clip:** a clear line that fits the sign: "Good morning! Chai Corner is open at 7 AM. Come on in!" Nobody asked for this one either.

So always write the sound you want: the exact words, or only background sounds.

### The scorecard

| Test | Kling | Omni | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality | more like a photo | steam looks drawn | Kling, by a little |
| Motion | face changes a little | face changes a little | A tie |
| Faces | the stream drifts | the pour stays in | Omni, by a little |
| Text | spelled right | spelled right, plus a cup | A tie |
| Sound | made-up language | clear words | Omni |

There is no single winner. Each tool is better for some shots.

## Worked example

The four prompts as typed into Flow (the same words as the Kling prompts from Module 5). All 9:16 and 5 seconds unless noted.

Test 1, quality (text only, 720p, 15 credits):

```text
A steel kettle pours a long stream of hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter at a roadside chai stall. Steam rises from the glass. Early morning, soft golden sunlight. Close-up, slow push in. Realistic, shallow depth of field. No text.
```

Test 2, motion (start picture of Meera, 360p, 5 credits):

```text
The camera spins fast around her in a full circle while she dances, swings the kettle and pours chai. Then the camera zooms quickly into her face and pulls back again.
```

Test 3, faces (start picture of Meera pouring chai, 360p, 5 credits):

```text
The woman in the yellow apron keeps pouring a thin stream of hot chai from the kettle into the glass. Steam rises from the glass. She looks up at the camera and smiles warmly. Her hair moves a little in the breeze. Slow push in. Keep her face and clothes the same. No text.
```

Test 4, text (text only, 16:9, 360p, 5 credits):

```text
A young woman holds a chalkboard sign that reads CHAI CORNER OPEN 7 AM and waves, natural daylight
```

## Common mistakes

- **An unfair test.** Change the words, the picture or the length and you are not comparing the tools any more.
- **Trusting one test.** Each tool wins some shots and loses others.
- **Leaving the sound out of the prompt.** Both tools add sound by themselves: Kling made speech in no real language, Omni spoke a line nobody wrote.
- **Fast camera moves on a face.** The face changes a little in both tools.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: run one of your own prompts in both tools, and score the two clips on quality, motion, faces, text and sound.
2. Add a sound line to the Test 4 prompt so the clip has only background sounds and no voice.
3. Which tool would you pick for a close-up of chai being poured, and which for a person who must speak a clear line?

## Answers

1. Good looks like: the same words, the same start picture and the same length in both; a 5-row scorecard like the one above.
2. For example, add: `Background: a quiet street, birds far away. No voice. No music.` (Write the sound you want, as in M07-L02.)
3. From the scorecard: the pour close-up leans Kling (more like a real photo); the speaking person leans Omni (clear words).
