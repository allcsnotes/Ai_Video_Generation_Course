# M08-L03 · A Decision Chart

> Video: Module 8, Lesson 3. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

You know two video tools now: Kling and Omni. But which one should you open for your next shot? This lesson gives you a simple chart: four questions, and you have your answer.

The chart is in the download [kling-vs-omni-decision-chart.md](../../downloads/kling-vs-omni-decision-chart.md), with an empty table for your own shots.

## What you'll learn

- The four questions, in order, and the rule "stop at the first yes"
- Which tool each answer sends you to, and its settings
- What to do when no answer is yes
- How the chart picks the tool for four real Chai Corner ideas
- That Kling's **Omni reference** mode is not the Omni model in Flow

## Key ideas

### Four questions, in order

Ask the questions in this order, and **stop at the first yes**.

| # | Question | If yes, use | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does someone speak? | Omni | write the exact words in quotes |
| 2 | Must a person hold your product? | Kling | one picture of each |
| 3 | Is it a close-up of food or a product, with no people? | Kling 4.0 Flash | it looked the most real |
| 4 | Does the shot need more than 8 seconds? | Flow + Extend | make 8 s, then extend |

No yes at all? Use the tool that has credits left, and test cheap first.

```text
Q1 Someone speaks?            yes -> Omni (exact words in quotes)
   no
Q2 Person holds the product?  yes -> Kling (one picture of each)
   no
Q3 Food or product close-up?  yes -> Kling 4.0 Flash
   no
Q4 More than 8 seconds?       yes -> Flow + Extend
   no
   -> the tool with credits left, test cheap first
```

### The settings for each answer

| If | Use | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Someone speaks | Omni | 9:16 · 8 s · test at 360p · final at 720p |
| Person + product | Kling 4.0 Flash | Omni reference · 2 pictures · 5 s |
| Food close-up | Kling 4.0 Flash | 720p · 5 s · 30 credits each |
| Longer than 8 s | Flow | make 8 s first, then extend 4 s at a time |

And in Flow, always set **x1**: x2 costs double.

## Worked example

The video tries the chart on four Chai Corner ideas, each with a clip made earlier in the course.

### Idea 1 · Chai pouring into a glass, close-up

- Does someone speak? **No.** A person with a product? **No.** A close-up of food? **Yes.**
- So: **Kling 4.0 Flash**, 720p, 5 s, 9:16, 30 credits. The clip is the one from Module 5.

### Idea 2 · Meera says a welcome line to the camera

- Does someone speak? **Yes.** Stop there: **Omni**.
- Start from her picture and write her line in quotes: "Welcome to Chai Corner. Fresh chai, every morning!" 360p test, 8 s. The clip is the one from Module 7.

### Idea 3 · Meera holds up our tea tin next to her face

- Does she speak? **No.** Does she hold a product? **Yes**: **Kling**.
- Upload her picture and the tin picture. Kling calls this mode **Omni reference**. It is part of Kling, not the Omni model in Flow. 5 s, 30 credits. The clip is the one from Module 6.

### Idea 4 · A 12 second shot

Meera sips her chai, then waves and says: "See you tomorrow!"

- Does she speak? **Yes**, so **Omni**. And it is longer than 8 seconds: make 8, then extend by 4.
- The clip is the one from Module 7: one shot, 12 seconds, **12 credits at 360p** (8 for the first 8 seconds, 4 for the extend).

| Idea | First yes | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · pour close-up | Q3 | Kling 4.0 Flash |
| 2 · welcome line | Q1 | Omni 1.2 Flash |
| 3 · holds the tin | Q2 | Kling 4.0 Flash, Omni reference |
| 4 · 12 second shot | Q1 (and Q4) | Omni 1.2 Flash in Flow, then Extend |

## Common mistakes

- **Asking the questions out of order.** A talking shot that also needs a product still stops at Q1.
- **Mixing up the two "Omnis".** Omni reference is a Kling mode; Omni 1.2 Flash is the model in Google Flow.
- **Making one long clip instead of extending.** Make 8 seconds first, then extend 4 at a time.
- **Leaving Flow on x2.** Always set x1.
