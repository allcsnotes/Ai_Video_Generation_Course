# M08-L02 · Cost per Usable Second

> Video: Module 8, Lesson 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

This Kling clip cost 30 credits on the button. But it really cost 120. The button shows the price of one try, and every retake costs credits too.

## What you'll learn

- The price on the button in Kling and in Flow, per second
- Why Kling credits and Flow credits are not the same money, and what each plan gives you
- The real price: **cost per usable second** = all the credits you spent ÷ the seconds you kept
- Two real examples from the course: one Kling clip that cost 4 times the button price, one Omni clip that cost 2 times
- The x2 trap in Flow, and five ways to pay less

## Key ideas

### The price on the button

| Tool and setting | Button price | Per second |
|---|---|---|
| Kling 4.0 Flash · 720p · 5 s | 30 credits | 6 Kling credits |
| Omni in Flow · 720p · 8 s | 24 credits | 3 Flow credits |
| Omni in Flow · 360p | | 1 Flow credit |

But Kling credits and Flow credits are **not the same money**. So look at what each plan gives you:

| Plan | Credits a month | What that buys |
|---|---|---|
| Kling free | 66 | 2 Kling clips (5 s, 30 credits each) |
| Flow with Google AI Plus | 200 | 40 clips of 5 s at 360p |

Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.

### The real price

Not every clip comes out right. You keep some, and you throw some away. So count it like this:

```text
cost per usable second = all the credits you spent ÷ the seconds you kept
```

A **usable second** is a second of video you actually keep and use.

### The x2 trap in Flow

The outputs button in Flow starts at **x2**: two videos from one prompt. That is 48 credits instead of 24 (720p, 8 s). Click **x1**, unless you really want two to choose from.

### Five ways to pay less for each usable second

| # | Way | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test at 360p | 360p · 1 credit a second | a third of the 720p price |
| 2 | 720p once | the final clip only | when the prompt works |
| 3 | One clear action | She lifts the glass and smiles. | fewer surprises, fewer retakes |
| 4 | Write the sound | Sound: a kettle pours, birds. | no paying to fix it later |
| 5 | Save what worked | your prompt notebook | start from it next time |

## Worked example

### A Kling example (from Module 5)

The goal: Meera with a glass of chai, and no steam over her face. Kling 4.0 Flash, 720p, 5 s, 30 credits a try.

| Try | What came out |
|---|---|
| 1 | steam over her face |
| 2 | "no steam" written in, and even more steam |
| 3 | a new start picture: still some steam |
| 4 | clean, kept |

| Step | Number |
|---|---|
| 4 tries × 30 | 120 credits |
| Seconds kept | 5 s |
| 120 ÷ 5 | **24 credits a second** |
| The button said | 6 a second |

24 credits a second is **4 times** the price on the button.

### An Omni example (from Module 7)

A chai pour with sound, 8 seconds at 360p, 8 credits a try.

| Try | What came out |
|---|---|
| 1 | no sound words, and the sound was very faint |
| 2 | the sound written in, and it worked |

| Step | Number |
|---|---|
| 2 tries × 8 | 16 credits |
| Seconds kept | 8 s |
| 16 ÷ 8 | **2 credits a second** |
| The button said | 1 a second |

2 times the button price: one cheap retake.

Both examples are in the download [cost-per-usable-second.csv](../../downloads/cost-per-usable-second.csv), with empty rows for your own clips. Each column header says how to work it out.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading only the button.** One try is not the real price; retakes count too.
- **Comparing Kling credits with Flow credits directly.** They are different money: compare what each plan gives you.
- **Leaving Flow on x2.** It doubles the cost of every prompt.
- **Testing a new prompt at 720p.** 360p costs a third.
- **Not saving prompts that worked.** You pay again to rediscover them.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: for your next 3 clips, write down the credits and the seconds you keep, then work out your own cost per usable second (use the CSV).
2. You make 3 tries in Kling 4.0 Flash (5 s, 30 credits each) and keep one 5 s clip. What is the cost per usable second, and how many times the button price is it?
3. In Flow you test twice at 360p, 8 s, x1, then make the final once at 720p, 8 s, x1, and keep the 8 s final. What is the cost per usable second?

## Answers

1. Good looks like: three rows filled in, total credits ÷ seconds kept for each.
2. 3 × 30 = 90 credits ÷ 5 s = 18 credits a second: 3 times the button price of 6.
3. 2 × 8 = 16, plus 24 for the final = 40 credits ÷ 8 s = 5 Flow credits a second.
