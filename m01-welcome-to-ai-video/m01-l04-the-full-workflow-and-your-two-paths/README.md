# M01-L04 · The Full Workflow and Your Two Paths

> Video: Module 1, Lesson 4. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

One idea. Four tools. One finished ad. A café owner wants a 15 second ad for her morning chai, for a small café called Chai Corner. By the end of this course you can make it from your laptop.

Which tool do you open first? Not the video tool. You open Claude. Every good video starts as words, long before it moves.

## What you'll learn

- The four stations of the workflow and what goes in and out of each
- How one real ad moves through all four stations
- Why this order: mistakes get more expensive at every station
- Your two paths: ₹0, and ₹199 a month to add Veo
- The rules that come with free plans
- Three workflow mistakes

## Key ideas

### Four stations

| # | Station | Tool | In | Out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Writer | Claude | Your idea | A script, a list of shots, one prompt per shot |
| 2 | Photographer | ChatGPT | A prompt | A still picture: the first frame of each shot |
| 3 | Camera | Kling · Veo | A picture | A short moving clip |
| 4 | Editing room | CapCut | All the clips | One finished video, with music and text |

Words → pictures → clips → one video.

### Why this order?

Because mistakes get more expensive at every station.

| Station | What you do | Cost of a mistake |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Claude | Change a word | Free: rewrite 10 times a minute |
| 2 · ChatGPT | Make a picture | Cheap: see the look in seconds |
| 3 · Kling | Make a 5 second clip | 30 credits on a free account: the expensive step |

So you fix problems early, while they are cheap. Make a clip only when the words and the picture are right.

### Two paths

| | Path 1 · ₹0 | Path 2 · ₹199 |
|---|---|---|
| Claude | Free plan | Everything in path 1 |
| ChatGPT | Free plan, fewer images a day | |
| Kling | 66 free credits a month | |
| CapCut | Free desktop app | |
| Extra | | Veo: talking + sound, comes with Google AI Plus |
| Price | ₹0 | New users, intro offer ₹199 / month; after the offer ₹399 / month |

- 66 credits a month is about 2 short clips (2 × 30 credits), so plan every clip and test your ideas as pictures first.
- Prices shown are prices in India, November 2026. Check the current price before you pay.
- Every lesson shows the free way first. Only the Veo lessons need path 2.
- Later in the course (Module 7) the second path is used inside Google Flow, where the default model is Omni 1.2 Flash.

### Free plans come with rules

1. **Watermark.** Free clips can carry a logo, like the small Kling logo in the chai clip.
2. **Business use.** Some free plans limit how you can use the videos for business. Read the tool's rules before you sell a video.
3. **Prices change.** Credits and plans change often. Check today's price before you pay for anything.

The smart plan: learn for free, and upgrade only when your first client pays you.

## Worked example

The Chai Corner ad, through all four stations.

### Station 1 · Claude plans the ad

You type:

```text
A 15 second ad for Chai Corner, warm and cosy, for Instagram
```

Claude gives back a short script, 3 shots of 5 seconds each, and one prompt per shot:

| Shot | What we see | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · 5 s | A cup of chai with steam | close-up, clay cup, steam rising, warm morning light, slow push in |
| 2 · 5 s | A smiling customer holds the opening sign | young woman holds a chalkboard sign, waves, natural daylight |
| 3 · 5 s | The logo and "Open 7 AM" | Chai Corner logo on a warm brown background, soft glow |

### Stations 2, 3 and 4 · pictures, clips, edit

1. ChatGPT makes one picture for each shot. You look at them and keep the best.
2. Kling brings each picture to life. Steam rises. The customer waves.
3. In CapCut you join the clips, add soft music and the opening time, and export a tall 9:16 video.

Result: a tall video, ready for Reels. 15 seconds · 9:16.

## Common mistakes

- **Jumping straight into the video tool.** You burn your credits on guesses. Start with words, in Claude.
- **Expecting one tool to do everything.** Each tool has one job it does best.
- **Paying for every plan on day one.** Learn on the free path and upgrade only when you need it.

Words first, video last: Claude → ChatGPT → Kling → CapCut.

## Try it yourself
