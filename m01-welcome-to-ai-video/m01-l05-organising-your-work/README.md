# M01-L05 · Organising Your Work

> Video: Module 1, Lesson 5. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Ten clips in, and your Downloads folder looks like this:

```text
video (3).mp4
kling_1731402219.mp4
final_FINAL_2.mp4
Untitled.png
image (12).png
chai new.mp4
video (4).mp4
new prompt copy.txt
final_use_this.mp4
IMG_0457.png
```

Which one was the good chai shot? Which prompt made it? Nobody knows. Made it again? Then you paid credits twice.

What is the cheapest way to save credits? Not a better tool. Good organising. When you can find and reuse what worked, you never pay twice for the same clip.

## What you'll learn

- One folder per project, with five numbered folders inside
- A file name that explains itself
- The prompt notebook: the most valuable file you will own
- The credit tracker, and why free Kling credits must be planned
- Three organising mistakes

## Key ideas

### One folder per project

Make one folder for each project (here, Chai Corner). Inside it, make five folders in the same order as the workflow:

| Folder | What goes in | From |
|---|---|---|
| `01_script` | Script, shot list, prompts | Claude |
| `02_images` | Start frames | ChatGPT |
| `03_clips` | Video clips | Kling · Veo |
| `04_edit` | The edit project | CapCut |
| `05_export` | The finished video | Export |

- The numbers 01 to 05 keep the folders in that order.
- Every file goes into its folder the moment you download it. Not later. Right away.

### A name that explains itself

A good name tells you what is inside without opening the file. The pattern:

```text
project_shot_version_tool
chai_shot02_v03_kling.mp4
```

| Part | Example | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Project | `chai` | Which project |
| Shot | `shot02` | Which shot |
| Version | `v03` | Which try |
| Tool | `kling` | Which tool made it |

- Use small letters and underscores instead of spaces. Every computer and every tool can read that.
- Never call a file "final". There is always one more version. Use version numbers instead.

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| `Chai Shot 2 NEW.mp4` | `chai_shot02_v01_kling.mp4` |
| `final_FINAL_use this.mp4` | `chai_shot02_v04_kling.mp4` |

### Your prompt notebook

Every time you generate, write down the prompt, the tool and the settings. Then rate the result from 1 to 5, and write one line about what went wrong. A simple spreadsheet is enough. Google Sheets is free.

The notebook from the video (one row per generate):

| # | Prompt | Tool | Settings | What went wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | clay cup of masala chai, steam rising, warm morning light, slow push in | Kling 4.0 Flash | 5 s · 720p · 16:9 | steam a little fast |
| 2 | woman holds a chalkboard sign CHAI CORNER OPEN 7 AM, waves | Kling 4.0 Flash | 5 s · 720p · 16:9 | sign changes size |
| 3 | Chai Corner logo on a warm brown background, soft glow | ChatGPT | square image | nothing |

Each row also has a rating column with 1 to 5 stars. After a month you have your own library of prompts that work. That library is pure gold when clients arrive.

### The credit tracker

When you use more than one free account, it is easy to lose track of your credits. Keep one row for each clip: the date, the account, the credits it used, and what is left.

| Date | Account | Clip | Used | Left |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Nov | Account A | `chai_shot01_v01_kling.mp4` | −30 | 36 |
| 14 Nov | Account A | `chai_shot02_v01_kling.mp4` | −30 | 6 |
| 13 Dec | Account A | `chai_shot02_v02_kling.mp4` | −30 | 36 |
| 20 Dec | Account A | `chai_shot03_v01_kling.mp4` | −30 | 6 |

- Kling's free credits do not carry over. They expire after one month, so plan your clips and use them before the next month. That is why the tracker jumps back to 36 left in December: a new month gave 66 again.
- Before every clip, check the tracker. If you are low, test the idea as a picture first, and make the clip only when it looks right.

Download the tracker: [credit-tracker.csv](../../downloads/credit-tracker.csv). It adds a few columns (tool, plan, settings, keep?) so it works as a light prompt log too. The example rows are the video's numbers.

## Worked example

Saving one good chai shot, the organised way:

1. Kling finishes shot 2, try 3. You download it and rename it at once to `chai_shot02_v03_kling.mp4`.
2. It goes straight into `Chai Corner/03_clips`.
3. In the prompt notebook you add a row: the prompt, Kling 4.0 Flash, 5 s · 720p · 16:9, a rating, and one line on what went wrong.
4. In the credit tracker you add a row: the date, the account, −30, and what is left.

Next week, a client wants the same look. You open the notebook, copy the prompt, and pay once, not twice.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving everything in Downloads.** In a week you cannot find anything. Use one folder per project, five inside.
- **Not saving the prompt.** A clip comes out great and you can never make it again. Add one notebook row every time you generate.
- **Paying twice for a failed prompt,** because you forgot you already tried it. Check the notebook before you generate.

Ten minutes of organising saves hours and credits.
