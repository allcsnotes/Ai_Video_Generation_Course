# M09-L03 · Same Place, Same Look

> Video: Module 9, Lesson 3. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Look behind Meera in the six shots from the last lesson: a wooden kiosk, a tray, a busy street, a kitchen, and string lights at night. Meera stayed the same. Her stall did not: it looks like five different places. This lesson locks two more things: **the world** (the place) and **the look** (the light and colour).

## What you'll learn

- Why the place and the colour drift, just like faces
- How to make one picture of the place in Flow Image mode (Nano Banana, 0 credits)
- How to add the character and the place picture as ingredients
- The four-part prompt, with two fixed blocks: the place and the look
- How one word in the look line changes the whole look

## Before you start

- Flow, with the Meera character from lesson 2.
- The place picture uses Flow's **Image** setting with **Nano Banana 2.1**: **0 credits** in the video.
- Each video shot is **9:16 · 360p · 5 s · x1 = 5 credits**. The video makes three shots: **15 credits**.

## Follow along

### Why did the place change?

The prompts only said "her stall". They never said what the stall looks like. So the model guessed the place, a new guess in every shot, just like faces in lesson 1.

The light and colour drifted too: some shots are golden, some are hazy, and one is a blue evening.

| Lock | What it covers |
|---|---|
| The world | the place itself: the stall, the street, the props |
| The look | the light and the colour of every shot |

### 1 · Make one picture of the place

Make one picture of the place, with **no people** in it. In Flow, switch the settings to **Image**. Nano Banana makes it for 0 credits.

Describe everything you want to keep (prompt below):

- a blue wooden counter
- a yellow sign that says CHAI CORNER
- a green tarp roof
- a brass kettle
- a neem tree on the left

What you should see: the stall picture. This picture is now **the stall**, for every shot.

### 2 · Add the ingredients

Switch to video, **Ingredients** mode, and click the **+**.

1. Add **Meera** from **Characters**.
2. Switch to **Images**, pick the stall picture, and click **Add to prompt**.

What you should see: two pictures in the prompt box, Meera and the stall.

### 3 · Write the four-part prompt

| # | Part | Text | Changes? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shot + action | Medium shot. Meera pours hot chai from the brass kettle into small glasses on her blue counter, steam rising. | changes every shot |
| 2 | Meera | Meera: shoulder-length wavy dark hair, small gold hoop earrings, white shirt with rolled sleeves, mustard yellow apron. | copy word for word |
| 3 | The place | Place: her small chai stall on a quiet street corner: blue-painted wooden counter, yellow CHAI CORNER sign, green tarp roof, brass kettle on a small gas stove, neem tree on the left. | copy word for word |
| 4 | The look | Look: early morning, warm golden light, soft haze, photorealistic, warm golden colour grade, soft film grain. She does not speak. No captions. | copy word for word |

The place and the look are **fixed blocks**: copy them, word for word, into every prompt. Only the first part changes.

### 4 · Make the shots (5 credits each)

The video makes three shots this way: a medium, a close-up and a wide.

What you should see:

- The same blue counter, the same yellow sign, the same green roof, the same tree.
- The same warm morning light in all three. They cut together like one real morning.
- Bonus: the prompt said "She does not speak", and this time she did not.

### 5 · The look: one fixed line

The look is one fixed line at the end of every prompt:

| Part | Text |
|---|---|
| Time of day | early morning |
| Light | warm golden light, soft haze |
| Colour | warm golden colour grade |
| Style | photorealistic, soft film grain |

Change one word, and the whole look changes. The dusk shot from lesson 2 had a blue evening sky, so it looks like another film.

If you want a time change, make it on purpose. Then keep the new look for every shot in that part.


## Common mistakes

1. **Writing only "her stall".** The model guesses a new place every shot.
2. **People in the place picture.** Make it with no people, so it only sets the place.
3. **Rewriting the place or look blocks.** Copy them word for word; only the shot and action change.
4. **Changing the time by accident.** One word (like "dusk") changes the whole look.
5. **Checking only the face.** Check the background too.

## Your task

Make a picture of your own place, and two shots in it. Put them side by side: do they look like the same place?
