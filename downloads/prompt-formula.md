# The prompt formula

From the AI Video Creation course, Module 3, Lesson 4 (The Prompt Formula). Use it for every video prompt.

## The seven parts, in this order

| # | Part | The question it answers | Chai stall, shot 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subject | Who or what is in the shot? | the chai man |
| 2 | Action | What happens? | strikes a match, a blue flame bursts |
| 3 | Setting | Where are we? | a dark roadside chai stall |
| 4 | Camera | Shot size and move | extreme close-up, slow push in |
| 5 | Light | Time of day and mood | blue dawn, orange glow |
| 6 | Style | Real film, cartoon or clay? | photorealistic, 9:16, no text |
| 7 | Sound | What do we hear? | match strike, whoosh |

Write the answers in this order, as short sentences.

## The example from the video

```text
The chai man. He strikes a match. A blue flame bursts under the kettle. Dark roadside chai stall. Extreme close-up, slow push in. Blue dawn light, orange flame glow. Photorealistic, 9:16, no text. Sound: match strike, whoosh.
```

## Why this order

- Video models pay the most attention to the start of a prompt. Put who it is and what they do first.
- Camera, light and style come next. They shape how the shot looks.
- Sound goes last. Some tools (like Veo) make sound with the video; others ignore it. Sound is a bonus.

## Rules

- Keep it to about 40 to 60 words. A very long prompt can confuse the model.
- One action per prompt (one clip). "He pours, then he serves" is two clips.
- Describe your main character once, in detail (age, beard, glasses, clothes, one special detail), and copy the same words into every prompt.
- Use words a camera can see:

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| a nice morning | golden sunrise light through steam |
| a moving camera | a slow push in |
| good quality | photorealistic |
| a sign that says OPEN | no text (add it later in the edit) |

- Write short sentences, not a pile of keywords ("chai, morning, beautiful, cinematic, 4K").

## Fill it in

```text
1 Subject:
2 Action:
3 Setting:
4 Camera:
5 Light:
6 Style:
7 Sound:
```

Then join the seven answers into short sentences, count the words (40 to 60), and check that the character words are the same as in your other prompts.
