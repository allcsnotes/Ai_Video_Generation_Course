# Pricing Worksheet

From M15-L04 (Pricing as a Beginner). Work out your **floor**: the lowest price you will take for one video. Use your own numbers. The example numbers below are from the video and are examples only, not a suggested rate.

Freelance sites keep a share of every payment. Fees differ and change, so check each site's fee page before you set your price. Tool and credit prices also change: check what your credits cost you today.


## 5 · Write down what the price includes

```text
- One video
- Up to ___ seconds
- Shape: ___ (for example 9:16, vertical)
- Music and captions
- ___ rounds of changes (the video's example: two)
A further round of changes: extra
```

## 6 · Beginner rules

- Start a little low to win your first clients, but never below your floor.
- After the first free sample, do not work for free.
- Ask for half the payment before you start, and the rest before you send the final file.
- After every few happy clients, raise your price a little.
