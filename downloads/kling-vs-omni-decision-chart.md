# Kling vs Omni: decision chart

From M08-L03 · A Decision Chart. Ask the questions **in this order** and **stop at the first yes**.

## The four questions

| # | Question | If yes, use | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does someone speak? | Omni | write the exact words in quotes |
| 2 | Must a person hold your product? | Kling | one picture of each |
| 3 | Is it a close-up of food or a product, with no people? | Kling 4.0 Flash | it looked the most real |
| 4 | Does the shot need more than 8 seconds? | Flow + Extend | make 8 s, then extend |

No yes at all? Use the tool that has **credits left**, and **test cheap first**.

```text
Q1 Someone speaks?            yes -> Omni (exact words in quotes)
   no
Q2 Person holds the product?  yes -> Kling (one picture of each)
   no
Q3 Food or product close-up?  yes -> Kling 4.0 Flash
   no
Q4 More than 8 seconds?       yes -> Flow + Extend
   no
   -> the tool with credits left, test cheap first
```

## The settings for each answer

| If | Use | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Someone speaks | Omni | 9:16 · 8 s · 360p test · 720p final |
| Person + product | Kling 4.0 Flash | Omni reference · 2 pictures · 5 s |
| Food close-up | Kling 4.0 Flash | 720p · 5 s · 30 credits |
| Longer than 8 s | Flow | 8 s first · extend 4 s at a time |

In Flow, always set **x1**: x2 costs double.

Note: **Omni reference** is a mode inside Kling (you upload two pictures). It is not the Omni model in Google Flow.
