# Build plan template

Fill this in before you spend a single credit. It follows the path every build in Modules 12 and 13 uses: idea, tool, clips, credits, sound, edit, upload. Copy it into a notes app or print it.

Prices and plans change: check the current price of each tool before you buy.

## 1 · The idea

Write it in **one sentence**. If you cannot, the idea is not ready yet.

- Idea: ______________________________________________
- Platform and shape: Reel / Short / YouTube video · 9:16 or 16:9
- Length: ____ seconds
- Who is it for? ______________________________________

Examples from the course:

| Build | The idea, in one sentence |
|---|---|
| Build 1 | A rainy evening, and a hot glass of chai waiting at Chai Corner. |
| Build 2 | Chai Corner now sells its masala chai in a tin, so you can make it at home. |
| Build 5 | Ravi, Meera's oldest customer, arrives late and asks for one last glass. |

## 5 · The sound

- [ ] Voice: none / in the clip / AI Studio / my own
- [ ] Music or sound effect: name ____________ · link ____________
- [ ] Licence checked (free to use, not in YouTube's Content ID)
- [ ] Clip sound: keep (dialogue inside) / mute (V1 speaker or Mute filter)
- [ ] Music level under a voice: about -12 dB to -18 dB
- [ ] Fade in / fade out lengths: ____ s / ____ s

## 6 · The edit (Shotcut)

- [ ] Video mode: Vertical HD 30 fps (9:16) / HD 1080p 24 fps (16:9)
- [ ] Clips in order (open each, press A)
- [ ] Trimmed to the voice or the beat (S to split, X to ripple delete, I and O)
- [ ] Captions: Text: Simple, or Speech to Text, every line read and fixed
- [ ] Title on the first clip, Fade Out Video on the last
- [ ] Export: YouTube preset, green tick in Jobs

## 7 · Check and upload

- [ ] File size and length checked (for example 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 15 s)
- [ ] Watched every clip to the end; fixed anything that looks wrong
- [ ] Watched on a phone, with sound on and with sound off
- [ ] No real person made to say or do anything they did not
- [ ] Title and description say what it is, and that AI was used
- [ ] Audience set (made for kids or not)
- [ ] AI use / altered content: Yes, if it looks real
- [ ] First upload as **Private**; check it before anyone else sees it
