# Sound levels cheat sheet

From M10-L04 · Sound Effects and Levels. Keep this next to you when you mix an ad.

## The three layers, loud to quiet

| Layer | Level | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | On top | Always the clearest sound |
| Sound effects | A little lower than the voice | Short, each one on its action |
| Music and background | Well below the voice | Start about 12 dB below the voice |

A decibel (dB) is the unit for how loud a sound is.

## Starting point

- Music about **12 dB below the voice**.
- Cannot hear every word? Turn the **music** down more.
- When nobody is speaking, you can raise the music a little.
- **Ducking** = lowering the music under the voice, and bringing it back up when nobody speaks.

## Sound effects

- [ ] Searched for the action, not the feeling (for example `pouring tea`)
- [ ] No shield icon on Pixabay (shield = registered with Content ID)
- [ ] Link saved as proof
- [ ] Silence at the start of the file cut off, so the sound is not late
- [ ] Each effect lands exactly on its action in the picture
- [ ] Only the part you need, with a tiny fade at the end

## Music

- [ ] Free library track, license read, no shield
- [ ] A calm part with no big changes
- [ ] Fade in about 0.5 s, fade out about 1.5 s (M10-L03)
- [ ] Well below the voice

## Voice

- [ ] About 0.5 s of quiet at the start and the end (M10-L02)
- [ ] Every word clear

## Final check

- [ ] Played on a phone speaker, at a low volume
- [ ] Played again with earphones
- [ ] Any word hard to catch: lowered the music (not pushed the voice up)
- [ ] Not everything as loud as possible (YouTube turns very loud videos down anyway)
