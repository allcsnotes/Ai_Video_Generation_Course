# Start Frame Checklist

From Module 4 of the AI Video Creation course (M04-L01 and M04-L02). Use it before any image becomes the first frame of a clip.

A start frame (first frame) is the image your video begins from. It locks the face, the place and the light, so the video tool only has to add the motion. In a video, everything in the frame has to move, so fewer things means fewer mistakes.

## 1. Before you write the prompt

- [ ] I know the shape of my final video: 9:16 (Reels, Shorts, stories), 16:9 (YouTube, TV) or 1:1 (a feed post).
- [ ] I know the one moment this image shows (an image prompt is one frozen moment: no camera move, no sound).
- [ ] I know where the motion will go in the clip (for example, the chai pours down into the glass).

## 2. Write the prompt with the three rules

- [ ] **One clear subject**, a little to one side: on the left or right third of the frame, not dead in the centre.
- [ ] **Room for the motion**: the target on the other side, with empty space for the action.
- [ ] **A plain background**: softly blurred, no signs, no people.
- [ ] The shot and the light (for example: medium shot, eye level, warm morning light from the right).
- [ ] The shape at the end: `vertical 9:16` or `wide 16:9`.
- [ ] The last words: `no text`.

Fill-in formula:

```text
Create an image. [who], [what they look like]. [He / She] stands on the [left / right] third of the frame and [what they hold or do]. On the other side, [target], with empty space for [the action]. Plain, softly blurred background, no signs, no people. [shot], [light]. Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

## 3. Check the image (the five checks)

- [ ] **Right shape?** 9:16 for a Reel, 16:9 for YouTube.
- [ ] **Face clear?** The face is clear and sharp.
- [ ] **Hands right?** Count the fingers.
- [ ] **No strange text?** No odd letters and no fake logo.
- [ ] **Room for the motion?** The action has space. If the pot touched the edge, the motion would get cut.

## 4. Watch for the three first-frame mistakes

- [ ] The action does not leave the frame (the glass is not cut off).
- [ ] It is not framed too tight (nothing that moves touches an edge).
- [ ] The background is not busy (no signs, crowd or clutter).

## 5. Need another shape?

- [ ] Do not crop a wide image into a tall video. Open the image in ChatGPT, click **Resize** and pick the shape (Square, Portrait, Story, Landscape or Widescreen). It paints more of the scene and keeps the same person.

Note: the AI places things close to where you ask, not exactly. Close is fine, as long as the motion has room.
