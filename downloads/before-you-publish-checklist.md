# Before You Publish: Checklist

From Module 14 (Safety, Rules and Going Further). Go through every line before you post a video or send it to a client. If any answer worries you, do not post it.

This is not legal advice. Rules differ in each country, and tool terms change: check the current terms of your tools and the rules where you live.

## 1 · Real people and consent (M14-L01)

- [ ] Is a real person's face or voice in this video? If no, skip to section 2.
- [ ] I have their consent **in writing**, signed.
- [ ] The form says: what I will make, where it will be shown, for how long, and that they can say stop at any time.
- [ ] I kept a copy of the form.
- [ ] This video is the one job they agreed to (permission for one video is not permission for every video).
- [ ] Could anyone be fooled, or hurt? The answer is no.

Never, not even as a joke: intimate fakes of a real person (and never anything like that with a child), voice scams, fake news or a famous person saying what they did not say, fake endorsements with a celebrity's face, or mocking a friend, classmate or colleague.

## 2 · The AI label (M14-L02)

- [ ] Does it look or sound real? (a real person, a changed real event or place, a realistic scene that never happened, AI music as the main focus, a realistic AI voiceover)
- [ ] If yes, or if I am not sure, I will add the label.
- [ ] YouTube: YouTube Studio > Upload > Attributes > AI use > Yes.
- [ ] Instagram or Facebook: the AI label option in the settings, just before I share.
- [ ] My caption or description says "Made with AI".
- [ ] The tool watermark is still on. I have not removed, cropped, blurred or covered any label.

## 3 · Commercial use and the watermark (M14-L03)

Only for paid or business work.

- [ ] My plan allows commercial use. (Buying credits is not the same as a membership.)
- [ ] On a free plan, the watermark stays. If the client wants a clean video, I upgraded to a plan that removes it.
- [ ] I have done real creative work on top of the clips: my script, my voice, my edit, how I chose and arranged the clips.

## 4 · Music licence (M14-L03)

- [ ] No film songs or famous tracks.
- [ ] The music has a clear licence that allows this use.
- [ ] I am not selling the track on its own.
- [ ] I saved the licence page and the track name.

## 5 · Logos and brands (M14-L03)

- [ ] No real brand's logo, no famous cartoon character, no celebrity.
- [ ] Any client logo is used with their written permission.
- [ ] Practice work for an invented brand is called a "sample", not a paid job.

## 6 · Records

- [ ] I kept a record of the prompts, the licences, and the permissions for this video.
