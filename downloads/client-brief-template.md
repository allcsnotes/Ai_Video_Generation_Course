# Client Brief Template

From M15-L05 (The Client Workflow) and M15-L04 (Pricing as a Beginner). Ask these questions before you make anything, write the answers in one short message, and send it back so you both agree on the job.

## Step 1 · The brief questions

Copy and send:

```text
Hi [name], thank you! Before I start, a few quick questions:
1. What is the video for? (a launch, a sale, an event)
2. Where will it be posted? (Instagram, YouTube, a shop screen)
3. How long is it, and what shape? (like 15 seconds, 9:16)
4. What must it show? (the product, the logo, the price, the place)
```

## The brief, written back

Fill this in from their answers and send it back:

```text
For:
Where:
Size:
Show:
```

Example from the video:

```text
For: the new cardamom cake
Where: Instagram
Size: 15 seconds, 9:16
Show: the cake, the logo, the price
```

## What the price includes

Write it down, so a small job does not grow forever. Example from M15-L04:

```text
One reel · what the price includes
- One video
- Up to 15 seconds
- 9:16, vertical
- Music and captions
- Two rounds of changes
A third round of changes: extra
Extras (cost extra): faster delivery, more changes, another size
Payment: half before I start, the rest before the final file
```

## Permissions to check (Module 14)

- [ ] Their logo: written permission to use it
- [ ] Any real person in the video: written consent (what, where, how long, the right to say stop)
- [ ] My plan allows commercial use
- [ ] Licensed music only
