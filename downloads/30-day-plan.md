# Your 30-Day Plan

From M15-L07. Four weeks, one small step every day, to land your first paid video.

**The habit:** one hour every day. 30 minutes making videos, 30 minutes reaching out. Tick each day you do your hour.

The video gives one goal for each week. The day-by-day split below is a suggested way to reach it; move days around to fit your life.

## Week 1 · Practise (days 1 to 7)

Remake three builds with your own ideas: one Instagram reel, one product ad, one faceless video. Plan each one with Claude before you generate.

- [ ] Day 1 · Pick your three ideas. Plan the reel with Claude (shot list and prompts).
- [ ] Day 2 · Generate and edit the reel.
- [ ] Day 3 · Plan the product ad with Claude.
- [ ] Day 4 · Generate and edit the product ad (voice and music).
- [ ] Day 5 · Plan the faceless video with Claude.
- [ ] Day 6 · Generate and edit the faceless video (voice and captions).
- [ ] Day 7 · Watch all three. Note what you would do faster, and how many credits you wasted.

## Week 2 · Your portfolio (days 8 to 14)

Five best videos, every glitch fixed, a one line note each. A short showreel, and one shared Google Drive folder.

- [ ] Day 8 · Pick your five best: a 15 second reel, a product ad, a faceless vertical video, a talking character or short film, an explainer.
- [ ] Day 9 · Fix every glitch you can see (hands, labels, text).
- [ ] Day 10 · Fix the rest. Put your best shot in the first two seconds of each.
- [ ] Day 11 · Write a one line note for each: what it is, which tools, how long it took. Practice work is a "sample".
- [ ] Day 12 · Cut a 30 to 60 second showreel.
- [ ] Day 13 · Put everything in one Drive folder. Share > General access > Anyone with the link. Test the link.
- [ ] Day 14 · Make a simple page (optional): your name, the showreel at the top, your five videos, what you offer, how to contact you.

## Week 3 · Reach out (days 15 to 21)

Five first messages every day, using the four part message. Mix freelance sites, Instagram, and local shops. Keep your sheet up to date, and follow up once after three days.

The four parts: their name and something real about their business · one simple idea · a link to a short sample · one easy question.

- [ ] Day 15 · 5 messages. Start your sheet (who, when, what they said).
- [ ] Day 16 · 5 messages.
- [ ] Day 17 · 5 messages.
- [ ] Day 18 · 5 messages. Follow up once with anyone from Day 15 who has not replied.
- [ ] Day 19 · 5 messages. Follow-ups from Day 16.
- [ ] Day 20 · 5 messages. Follow-ups from Day 17.
- [ ] Day 21 · 5 messages. Read your sheet: which message got replies? Improve it.

## Week 4 · Your first paid video (days 22 to 30)

When someone says yes: work out your lowest price, send a clear offer (the price, what is included, when), then follow the workflow: brief, script, first cut, revisions, delivery.

- [ ] Day 22 · Work out your floor price (see the pricing worksheet).
- [ ] Day 23 · Keep reaching out. Follow up once where due.
- [ ] Day 24 · Someone said yes? Send the brief questions and a clear offer. Ask for half the payment.
- [ ] Day 25 · Send the script and shot list for approval, before you spend a single credit.
- [ ] Day 26 · Make the clips and edit a first cut. Send a small draft preview. Ask for all changes in one message.
- [ ] Day 27 · Revision round 1.
- [ ] Day 28 · Revision round 2 (a third round costs extra).
- [ ] Day 29 · Ask for the rest of the payment. Export in full quality, every size you agreed. Share in a Drive folder. Keep your own copy.
- [ ] Day 30 · Look back at the month. Plan the next 30 days.

If nobody has said yes yet, that is normal. Improve your message, and keep going.
