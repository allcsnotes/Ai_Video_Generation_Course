# Export checklist

From M11-L06 · Export Settings and Quality Checks. The video exports from Shotcut (free, offline); the same checks work in any editor.

## Before you edit

- [ ] Shape set in the project's Video mode:
  - Reels, Shorts, TikTok: tall, 9:16, 1080 × 1920
  - Normal YouTube video: wide, 16:9, 1920 × 1080

## Export settings

- [ ] File type: MP4
- [ ] Video codec: H.264
- [ ] Frame rate: 30 fps
- [ ] Shotcut: Export > Presets > YouTube (sets MP4 and H.264; also works for Reels and Shorts)
- [ ] Video tab: Resolution and frame rate match the project
- [ ] Codec tab: Quality raised to 70% (apps squeeze every upload again, so start sharp)
- [ ] Audio tab: AAC, 384k
- [ ] Export Video/Audio, file named, saved

## Check the real file

- [ ] Jobs panel shows a green tick
- [ ] Right click the job > Open > Properties shows the right size and 30 fps

## Watch it, start to end

- [ ] No blurry or blocky patches
- [ ] No captions cut off at the edges
- [ ] Sound not too quiet or too loud
- [ ] Every word clear (see the sound levels cheat sheet)

## On your phone

- [ ] Sent to your phone
- [ ] Watched full screen, the way viewers will see it

## Before you post

- [ ] Music and sound effects from a free library, license saved as proof (M10-L03)
- [ ] Any AI label the platform asks for is left on (Module 14 covers the rules)
