# M15-L07 · Your 30-Day Plan

> Video: Module 15, Lesson 7. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

You have all the skills now. This last lesson turns them into a plan, so in 30 days you can land your first paid video. Four weeks, and one small step every day.

## What you'll learn

- Week 1 · Practise: remake three builds with your own ideas
- Week 2 · Your portfolio: five videos, a showreel, and a Drive folder
- Week 3 · Reach out: five first messages every day
- Week 4 · Your first paid video: price it, follow the workflow, deliver
- The one habit that makes the plan work

## Key ideas

### Week 1 · Practise

- Remake three of the builds from this course, **with your own ideas** (remake them, do not copy them):
  - one Instagram reel
  - one product ad
  - one faceless video
- **Plan each one with Claude** before you generate (Module 3).
- By the end of the week, you will be faster, and you will waste fewer credits.

### Week 2 · Your portfolio

- Make your **five best videos**, fix every glitch, and add a **one line note** to each.
- Cut a **short showreel**, and put everything in **one shared Google Drive folder**.

(How: M15-L02.)

### Week 3 · Reach out

- Send **five first messages every day**, using the **four part message**: their name and something real, one idea, a sample, one easy question.
- Mix the three places: **freelance sites**, **Instagram**, and **local shops** near you.
- Keep your **sheet** up to date, and **follow up once after three days**.

(How: M15-L03.)

### Week 4 · Your first paid video

- When someone says yes, **work out your lowest price** (your floor, M15-L04), and send a **clear offer**: the price, what is included, and when.
- Then follow the workflow (M15-L05): the brief, the script, the first cut, revisions, and delivery.
- If nobody has said yes yet, that is normal. Improve your message, and keep going.

### One habit: one hour a day

One habit makes the plan work. **Spend one hour every day, and track it.**

| Every day · 60 minutes | |
|---|---|
| 30 minutes | make videos |
| 30 minutes | reach out |

Tick each day you do your hour.

## Common mistakes

- **Copying the builds exactly in Week 1.** Use your own ideas, so the portfolio is yours.
- **Skipping the plan with Claude.** Planning first is how you waste fewer credits.
- **Sending one message and waiting.** Five a day, every day in Week 3.
- **Following up again and again.** Once, after three days.
- **Giving up in Week 4 if nobody says yes.** That is normal. Improve your message, and keep going.
- **Doing nothing for a few days.** One hour a day, every day: 30 minutes making, 30 minutes reaching out.
