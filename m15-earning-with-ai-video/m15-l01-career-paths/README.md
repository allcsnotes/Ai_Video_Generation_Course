# M15-L01 · Career Paths

> Video: Module 15, Lesson 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

You can now make reels, product ads, short films, explainers, and music videos. In this module, you turn those skills into income. This lesson shows five ways to earn with AI video, and how to choose your first one.

## What you'll learn

- Five career paths: freelancing, ads for local businesses, UGC-style ads, a faceless YouTube channel, and reels for brands
- How each one pays, and what it needs from you
- The honesty rules for UGC-style ads
- What YouTube needs before a channel can earn from ads
- Which path to start with

## Key ideas

### Path 1 of 5 · Freelancer

- You make videos for **clients you find online**, on sites like **Fiverr** and **Upwork**.
- You are **paid for each video**: one job, one payment.
- It is a good place to start, because each job is small and clear.
- But you need a **portfolio** (a small set of your best videos), and you need to **reply fast**.

The portfolio and finding clients are the next two lessons.

### Path 2 of 5 · Ads for local businesses

- Cafes, gyms, salons, and shops near you need short videos, but most of them cannot shoot or edit. So they need you.
- The chai ad we made in **Build 2** is exactly this kind of work. A shop owner sees the value in five seconds.
- You can charge **for each ad**, or for a **small monthly package**.

### Path 3 of 5 · UGC-style ads

- **UGC** means **user generated content**: ads that look like a normal person talking about a product, filmed on a phone.
- With AI, be careful. **Never present an AI person as a real customer giving a real review.** That misleads buyers.
- Use this style for clear product demos, keep the AI label on, and follow the rules from Module 14.

### Path 4 of 5 · A faceless YouTube channel

- You post explainers or fact videos with AI pictures and a voice, like **Build 3** and **Build 7**. You never show your face.
- But the money is slow. To earn from ads (the YouTube Partner Program), you need:

| Option | What you need |
|---|---|
| Long videos | 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours in 12 months |
| YouTube Shorts | 1,000 subscribers and 10 million Shorts views in 90 days |

- And YouTube says your content must be **original**, not mass-produced or repetitive. So every video needs real research and care.

### Path 5 of 5 · Reels for brands

- Small brands need fresh Instagram reels every week.
- You make a set, like **8 reels a month**, for **one monthly fee**.
- This gives you **steady income**, because the same client pays you every month.

## Worked example: which one first?

| Path | First money | Steady money |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancing | Fast | Medium |
| Local business ads | Fast | Medium |
| Reels for brands | Medium | High |
| Faceless channel | Slow | Can grow for years |

Local ads and freelancing bring the fastest first money. Reels for brands bring steady money. A channel is slow, but it can grow for years.

**A good plan:** start with local ads or freelancing, and build a channel on the side.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting with only a channel and expecting quick money.** The ad money needs the YouTube thresholds above first.
- **Faking a customer review with an AI person.** It misleads buyers. Use clear product demos, with the AI label on.
- **Mass-producing the same channel video.** YouTube wants original content, not repetitive videos.
- **Trying all five paths at once.** Pick one to start.
