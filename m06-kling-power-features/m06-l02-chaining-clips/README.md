### The idea: one picture links two clips

Kling 4.0 Flash has no Extend button. So we build the longer shot ourselves.

- A **frame** is one still picture from a video.
- The trick is one picture: the **last frame** of clip one. We use it as the **first frame** of clip two.
- So clip two starts from the same pose, the same light and the same face. Played back to back, there is no jump.

`5 s + 5 s = a 10 second shot`

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open First frame, then Asset

Open **Generate**, with **Video Generation** and **Kling 4.0 Flash**. Click **First frame**, then **Asset**.

### Step 2 · Open Extract Frames

Open the **Extract Frames** tab. It shows the videos you made in Kling.

### Step 3 · Click clip one

Click clip one. A new window opens, with a slider under the video.

### Step 4 · Keep the slider at the end, then Confirm

The slider picks the frame. It starts at the very end, which is exactly what we want. Click **Confirm**.

### Step 5 · Confirm again

The frame is now marked **Video Frame**. Click **Confirm** again, and it becomes the first frame.

Click path: `Generate > Video Generation > Kling 4.0 Flash > First frame > Asset > Extract Frames > clip one > slider at the end > Confirm > Confirm`

### Step 6 · Write the next move

Describe only what happens next: she takes one small sip, then lowers the glass and smiles. Then copy the style words from clip one, word for word: keep her face, hands and clothes the same, the air stays clear and still, and keep it slow and gentle.

| | Clip one prompt | Clip two prompt |
|---|---|---|
| New: what happens | She slowly lifts the glass of chai and smiles. | She takes one small sip of the chai, then lowers the glass and smiles warmly. |
| Camera | The camera slowly pushes in. One gentle move only. | The camera stays still. |
| Same speed | Subtle, natural motion. | Slow, gentle motion. |
| Copied style words | Keep her face, hands and clothes the same. The air stays clear and still. No text. | Keep her face, hands and clothes the same. The air stays clear and still. No text. |

Same words, same look. That is what makes two clips feel like one.

### Three rules for a smooth join

A chain can still feel wrong if the motion changes at the join.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep the speed | Clip one slow and gentle? Say slow and gentle in clip two as well |
| 2 | Keep the camera calm | Clip one moves in very slowly, so clip two can hold still. A sudden spin or a fast zoom would jump |
| 3 | End clip one on a calm moment | A still pose makes a clean picture. A frame in the middle of a fast move is often blurry |

**Editing tip:** when you join them in an editor, cut the first frame of clip two. It is the same picture as the last frame of clip one, so it would freeze for a moment.

## Prompts used

Clip one (from M05-L06):

```text
She slowly lifts the glass of chai and smiles. The camera slowly pushes in. One gentle move only. Subtle, natural motion. Keep her face, hands and clothes the same. The air stays clear and still. No text.
```

Clip two (first frame = the last frame of clip one):

```text
She takes one small sip of the chai, then lowers the glass and smiles warmly. The camera stays still. Slow, gentle motion. Keep her face, hands and clothes the same. The air stays clear and still. No text.
```

## Common mistakes

1. **New style words.** Change the words for the look, and the light or her clothes may change too.
2. **Moving the slider by mistake.** Clip two then starts from an earlier moment, and the story jumps back.
3. **A very long chain.** Each clip copies the one before, so small changes add up. After a few clips, check her face against your first picture.

The fix: same style words, slider at the end, check the face.

## Try it yourself

## Recap

- To make a longer shot, chain clips: the last frame of one clip is the first frame of the next.
- In Kling: First frame, Asset, Extract Frames, pick the clip, and keep the slider at the end.
- Keep the same style words, the same speed and a calm camera.
