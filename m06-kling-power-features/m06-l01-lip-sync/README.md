### What is lip sync?

Lip sync means the mouth moves with the sound. Every sound needs its own mouth shape:

| Sound | Mouth shape |
|---|---|
| M | lips close |
| A | mouth opens |
| O | lips go round |

Kling gives you two ways to do it:

| Way | You give it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Avatar | one picture and one line of speech | Kling makes the video and the voice: a talking clip |
| 2 · Lip Sync tool | a clip you already made, plus a voice | it moves the lips to match (it changes only the mouth) |

We start with **Avatar**. It needs only a picture, so it is the easiest way to test.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Choose Avatar

Open **Generate**. Click the type menu, and choose **Avatar**.

What you should see: two buttons appear, **Character** and **Speech**.

### Step 2 · Add the character

Click **Character**, and upload your picture. Pick one where the face is clear and nothing covers the mouth.

### Step 3 · Pick a voice

Click **Speech**, and choose **Voice**. Kling has ready voices: men, women, young and old.

Press play to hear one first. The video picks **Diana**. You can also set the speed, and a feeling, like happy or sad. (The video kept Speech Rate 1x and Neutral.)

### Step 4 · Write the line

Now two boxes appear.

1. In **Speech**, type the exact words she will say. Keep it short: one or two sentences, about five seconds.
2. In **Character Performance**, say how she acts: she looks at the camera, speaks warmly, and holds still.

The two texts from the video are in "Prompts used" below.

### Step 7 · Check it like a director

| # | Question | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do the lips match the words? | On sounds like M and P, the lips close |
| 2 | Is it still the same person? | Her face does not change while she talks |
| 3 | Does the voice fit her? | A friendly young woman selling chai needs a warm, bright voice |

One small problem in the video: we asked her to hold still, but the pouring keeps going for a moment. The picture started in the middle of an action.

So for talking clips, start from a calm picture: face to the camera, hands still.

## Prompts used

Speech (the exact words she says):

```text
Fresh masala chai, made with love. Come try a glass at Chai Corner!
```

Character Performance (how she acts):

```text
She looks at the camera and speaks warmly, with a small smile. She holds the kettle still. The camera stays still.
```

## Common mistakes

1. **A long speech in one clip.** Keep each clip to one or two sentences, and join the clips later.
2. **A hidden mouth.** A face that is turned away, or covered by hair, a hand or steam. Kling must see the mouth.
3. **Short forms.** A voice may read "Rs" letter by letter. Write the full word, the way it is spoken: rupees.

The fix: short lines, a clear mouth, words written as spoken.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: make Meera, or your own character, say one short line about your product.
2. Rewrite this line the way it is spoken: "Chai Rs 20, open 6am-11pm."
3. Look at your picture before you spend credits. Is the face to the camera, the mouth clear, and the hands still?

## Recap

- Avatar turns one picture and one line into a talking clip.
- Use a clear, calm picture that faces the camera, and a voice that fits.
- Keep the line short. Then check the lips, the face and the voice.
