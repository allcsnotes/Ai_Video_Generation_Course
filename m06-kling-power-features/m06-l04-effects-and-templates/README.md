# M06-L04 · Effects and Templates

> Video: Module 6, Lesson 4. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Some clips need no prompt at all. Pick an effect, add one picture, and Kling does the rest. Here, Meera turns into a crochet doll in a few seconds. Fun to watch, and quick to make.

## What you'll learn

- What a template (an effect) is, and the trade you make when you use one
- How to find Creative Effects in Kling and pick one
- What picture an effect needs, and what it costs at 720p and 1080p
- What changed in the video's result, and why the product label matters
- When to use an effect, and when not to
- Three mistakes with effects

## Before you start

- **Account:** your free Kling account.
- **One picture:** one clear picture of one person or one pet. The video uses a still of Meera holding the tin, from the last lesson.
- **Cost:** an effect costs more than a normal clip. In the video: **720p = 45 credits**, **1080p = 75 credits**. Test at 720p first. Prices change: read the number on the Generate button before you press it.

### A template is a ready recipe

A **template** is a recipe that Kling already wrote. The motion, the style and the camera are all fixed. You give it only one picture. You cannot change the story with words.

| | With a prompt | With a template |
|---|---|---|
| Story | you decide what happens | fixed |
| Motion | you decide how she moves | fixed |
| Camera | you decide how it films | fixed |
| Style | you decide how it looks | fixed |

With a prompt, you decide what happens. With a template, Kling decides. That is the trade: fast and fun, but you give up control.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open Creative Effects

Open **Generate**, then **Tools**, then **Creative Effects**. A gallery of effects opens.

Each picture in the gallery shows what the effect does. There are sports trends, festival effects like Christmas, and style changes like **Crochet Diary**.

### Step 2 · Pick an effect

Click one to pick it. The video picks **Crochet Diary**, which turns a person into a knitted doll.

### Step 3 · Add one picture

Click the **+** button, then **Upload**. Kling asks for one clear picture of one person or one pet.

The video uses a still of Meera holding the tin, from the last lesson.

### Step 4 · Pick the quality

| Quality | Credits |
|---|---|
| 1080p | 75 |
| 720p | 45 |

Test at **720p** first.

### Step 5 · Generate

Press **Generate**, and wait a few minutes. **This spends 45 credits at 720p.**

Click path: `Generate > Tools > Creative Effects > Crochet Diary > + > Upload > 720p > Generate`

### Step 6 · Check what changed

The result comes with the music the effect adds.

| What carried over | What changed |
|---|---|
| her hair | the tin became a knitted jar |
| her earrings | the label now says chai corner in small knitted letters |
| the apron colour | the real tin is gone |

It looks great, and it grabs attention. But it also costs 45 credits, more than a normal clip, and the template decides the length: 13 seconds here.

### When to use an effect

| Use one for | Skip it when |
|---|---|
| fun posts | the product must look exact |
| festivals: a Christmas effect in December | your label, your logo and your packaging matter (they can change) |
| trends people already know | it is the whole ad: anyone can use the same template, so it will not look like your brand |
| a quick hook to stop people scrolling | |

A good mix: one short effect as the **hook**, then your own clips with the **real product**.

## Prompts used

None. A template has no prompt box: you upload one picture and Kling uses its own fixed recipe.

## Common mistakes

1. **A busy picture.** Kling wants one person or one pet. A crowd or a busy table confuses it.
2. **Paying for 1080p first.** Test at 720p, and only pay more for an effect you will really share.
3. **Trusting the label.** Check every word on your product after the effect, before you post.

The fix: one clear subject, 720p first, check the label.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: try one effect on a picture of your character. Then check what changed: the face, the clothes and any product.
2. You are making a Diwali post and a product ad for the same brand. Which one gets an effect?
3. Plan a 15 second Reel that uses an effect the right way.

## Recap

- A template is a ready recipe: one picture in, a fixed effect out.
- Find them in Tools, then Creative Effects. Test at 720p, for 45 credits.
- Use effects for hooks and festivals, not when the product must look exact.
