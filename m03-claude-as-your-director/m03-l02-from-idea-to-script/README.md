# M03-L02 · From Idea to Script

> Video: Module 3, Lesson 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

On Instagram, people decide in about two seconds if they keep watching or scroll away. Which start would you keep watching?

- **Start A:** "Hello everyone, welcome to my chai stall."
- **Start B:** a black screen, then someone strikes a match, and a blue flame bursts.

Start B. It makes you curious, and you want to see what comes next. That first moment is called the **hook**.

## What you'll learn

- What a script for an AI video is: a simple table, not a page of dialogue
- How to write a hook for the first 2 seconds, and three hook types that work

## Key ideas

### A script is a simple table

For an AI video, a script is not a page of dialogue. For each part you write four things:

| Time | What we see | Text on screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Dark street. A match strikes. A blue flame bursts. | "6:59 AM" | strike, whoosh |
| 6–12 s | He crushes ginger and cardamom into the chai. | "Fresh ginger." | crushing |
| 12–18 s | The high pour. First sunlight through the steam. | "We're open." | pour, music |

- Every row becomes one **shot** later, and one shot becomes one **clip** of about 5 to 8 seconds.
- So the script decides how many clips you need, and how many credits you will spend. More rows = more clips = more credits. The script sets your budget.

### The hook: the first 2 seconds

A good hook puts a question in the viewer's head: What is this? What happens next?

| # | Hook type | How | In the chai Reel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mystery | Start dark and quiet. Show only a small part. | The match strike |
| 2 | Best moment first | Open in the middle of the most beautiful shot. Hard to scroll past. | The slow motion pour |
| 3 | Talk to the viewer | The camera is the viewer. Something comes toward you. | He pushes a glass of chai toward you: "Pehli chai?" |

The hook to avoid: a logo, a slow intro, or "Hello everyone". Nothing happens, so people scroll.

### A story in three acts

| Act | Job | In the chai Reel (60 s version) |
|---|---|---|
| Act 1 · 0–15 s | Set the scene: who is this, where are we? | A dark, quiet street. One man already at work. |
| Act 2 · 15–38 s | The change: something builds up | The chai boils, the sky turns gold, he pours from high up. |
| Act 3 · 38–60 s | The payoff: the result and what to do | Customers arrive. The ending says: come early. |

A simple trick: make the light change with each act. Blue dawn, then gold sunrise, then full sun. The viewer feels the story with no words needed.

### 30 or 60 seconds?

| Length | Shots | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 30 seconds | About 5 shots of 6 seconds | A hook, a short middle, a quick ending |
| 60 seconds | About 10 shots | More detail inside each act, not more ideas |

- At 60 seconds, add a **second hook** around the 15 second mark, when many people start to drift away. In our script, the chai nearly boils over.
- Count the cost. A 5 second Kling clip is 30 credits:

| Length | Clips | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| 30 s | 5 | 150 |
| 60 s | 10 | 300 |

300 credits is more than four months of free credits: the free Kling plan gives 66 credits a month, and they expire after a month. Plan every clip, and check the current free credits in your own account.

Start with 30 seconds. It costs less, and short Reels are often watched to the end.

## Worked example

The chai stall Reel, asked to the AI Video Director project from the last lesson.

1. Open the chai stall chat inside your AI Video Director project.
2. Type the request below and press Enter.

```text
Now help me with the script only. Give me 3 different hook ideas for the first 2 seconds. Then write a 60-second version of this reel in 3 acts, with the time for each part.
```

What came back:

- **3 hooks**, each with why it works: 1 · mystery (the flame), 2 · satisfying (the slow motion pour), 3 · direct (the POV hand-off, "Pehli chai?"). The script uses Hook 1.
- **Act 1 · The quiet · 0 to 15 s**, with what we see, the text and the sound for each part.
- **Act 2 · The brew · 15 to 38 s**, including the boil over moment that brings attention back.
- **Act 3 · The city wakes up · 38 to 60 s**, ending with a clear call to action: fresh chai from 7 AM. Come early.
- Claude also saved this script in the project, so the next lesson can turn it into shots.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Bad | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Too many ideas | Chai, snacks, prices, our story and a contest | One message: fresh chai from 7 AM |
| Too much text on screen | "Welcome to our stall, we serve fresh chai every morning" | "7:00 AM. We're open." (about five words) |
| No ending | The video just stops | Come early + the location (visit, follow or buy) |

One message, short text, a clear ending.
