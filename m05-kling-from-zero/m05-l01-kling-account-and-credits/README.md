# M05-L01 · Kling Account and Credits

> Video: Module 5, Lesson 1. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

This is the Kling home screen. Every clip you make here costs **credits** (Kling's own currency: each clip uses some). One 5 second clip can cost 30 credits, or 50. It depends on one menu. Pick the wrong one, and your free credits are gone after one clip.

## What you'll learn

- How to sign in to Kling for free, with no card
- What each part of the Kling dashboard does
- Where your credits are, where they come from, and two rules about them
- What each Kling model costs, read off the Generate button
- How to plan your 66 free monthly credits
- Three credit mistakes

## Before you start

- **Account:** a free Kling account at `kling.ai`. No card and no payment.
- **Cost of this lesson:** nothing. You only look at menus and prices. You never press **Generate**, so no credits are spent.
- **Free credits:** on the free plan you get **66** free monthly credits, and they expire after one month.
- **Prices change.** The numbers below are the ones the video read off the Generate button. Always read the number on the button before you press it, and check the current price before you buy any plan.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open Kling and sign in

1. Open `kling.ai` in your browser.
2. A gift box offers free credits. Click **Sign In to Claim Gift**, or the **Sign In** button on the left.
3. Pick one way to sign in: **Google**, **Apple** or **email**. Google is the fastest.

There is no card and no payment. A free account is all you need to start.

### Step 2 · Learn the dashboard

On the left are four places:

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| Home | where you start |
| Generate | your studio, with all your clips |
| Canvas | a big board for ideas |
| Assets | keeps your files |

In the middle is the **prompt box**. You type what you want to see, or add a picture with the **+** button.

Under it are four menus:

1. what to make
2. which model
3. the reference mode
4. the settings for quality, length and shape

On the right is the **Generate** button. This is the button that spends your credits.

### Step 3 · Open your credits

Your balance is the number on the left. Click it.

The panel shows your **remaining** credits. They come from three kinds:

`Remaining = Membership + Top-up + Bonus`

Open the **Obtained** tab. On the free plan, you get free monthly credits: **66**, and they expire after one month.

The course account in the video also has top-up credits, bought for testing. You do not need them.

Two good rules:

- Credits are taken when you press **Generate**.
- If a clip fails, you get them back.

Click path: `Kling > credit number on the left > Obtained`

### Step 4 · Compare the models

Open the model menu.

| Model | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Kling 4.0 | the best quality |
| Kling 4.0 Flash | the cheap and fast one |
| Kling 3.0 Omni | can also make sound |

Now read the price on the Generate button. These are the video's real prices, for 720p, 5 seconds, one clip:

| Model and setting | Credits |
|---|---|
| Kling 4.0 Flash · 720p · 5 seconds · 9:16 | 30 |
| Kling 4.0 · same settings | 50 |
| Kling 3.0 Omni · with sound | 45 |
| Kling 3.0 Omni · sound off | 30 |

Longer clips cost more: 10 seconds costs double. So read the number on the button before you press it.

### Step 5 · Plan your free credits

| With 66 free credits | You get |
|---|---|
| Kling 4.0 Flash, 30 per 5 second clip | two clips (60 credits), 6 left |
| Kling 4.0, 50 per 5 second clip | one clip, 16 left |

So test your idea in Flash first.

### Step 6 · Know what VIP means

A **VIP** badge means paid plans only, like 1080p, 4K, or more than one clip at a time.

Paid plans start at about $7 for the first month (the video's price; check the current price before you buy). You do not need one. In this course, we plan every clip, so nothing is wasted.

## Prompts used

None. This lesson does not make a clip.

## Common mistakes

1. **Not checking the model.** The menu may still be on Kling 4.0 from last time: 50 credits instead of 30 with Flash.
2. **A long clip by default.** 10 seconds costs double (60 instead of 30 with Flash). Start with 5.
3. **Generating just to test a prompt.** Five test clips cost 150 credits; a plan, then one clip, costs 30. Plan the shot first, with Claude, then generate once.

The fix: Flash, 5 seconds, and a plan before every clip.

## Try it yourself

1. The video's task: sign in, open your credit details, and check the **Obtained** tab. Then pick Kling 4.0 Flash, and read its price on the button.
2. Switch the model to Kling 4.0 and read the button again. Do not press it.
3. With 66 credits, how many 10 second Flash clips can you make?

## Recap

- Sign in with Google, Apple or email. No card needed.
- Your credits are on the left. Free monthly credits expire after a month, and failed clips are refunded.
- Flash costs 30 credits for 5 seconds. Kling 4.0 costs 50. Read the button every time.
