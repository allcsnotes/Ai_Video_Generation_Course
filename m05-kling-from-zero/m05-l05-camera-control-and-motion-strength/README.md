### Where are the camera controls?

Open the settings. You see **Mode, Duration, Ratio** and **Output**. That is all.

Older guides show a camera panel and a motion slider. In Kling today, they are gone. You control the camera, and how much things move, with words.

In **Tools**, **Motion Control** sounds like it, but it is different. It copies the moves from a video onto your character. It does not move the camera.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Name one camera move

These are the camera words from Module 2, Lesson 3. Now you type them.

| Move | What the camera does |
|---|---|
| Push in | moves closer |
| Pull back | moves away |
| Pan | turns left or right |
| Tilt | looks up or down |
| Orbit | circles around |
| Static | does not move at all |

The big rule: **one move per shot**. A push in, or an orbit. Never both.

### Step 2 · Set the motion dial with words

Think of a dial:

| Low | High |
|---|---|
| slowly, gently, subtle | fast, quickly, spins |

Every bit of movement means new picture detail that Kling has to invent. The more it invents, the more can go wrong:

- faces change
- hands bend
- objects appear

So keep the dial low. For a 5 second shot, one small action and one slow move is plenty.

### Step 5 · Prompt two: ask for one thing

Wait for the first clip (the free plan makes one at a time). Then type the calm prompt and press **Generate**. **This spends another 30 credits.**

```text
She slowly lifts the glass of chai and smiles. The camera slowly pushes in. One gentle move only. Subtle, natural motion. Keep her face, hands and clothes the same. No text.
```

It asks for one small action and one slow move, and it protects her: keep her face, hands and clothes the same. No text.


## Common mistakes

1. **Two moves at once.** An orbit plus a zoom is too much for 5 seconds. Pick one.
2. **Fast words.** Fast, quickly, spinning. Speed makes blur, and blur hides mistakes. Use slowly and gently.
3. **Vague words,** like "cinematic camera". Kling picks a move for you. Name the move yourself.

The fix: name one move, and say it slowly.

## Try it yourself

3. Which move would you name for a shot that should feel still and calm?

## Recap

- Kling has no camera panel. You control the camera with words.
- Name one move: push in, pull back, pan, tilt, orbit or static.
- Keep the motion low: slowly, gently, subtle. Less movement means fewer mistakes.
