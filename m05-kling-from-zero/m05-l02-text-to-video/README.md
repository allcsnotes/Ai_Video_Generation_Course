# M05-L02 · Text to Video

> Video: Module 5, Lesson 2. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

You type one paragraph, and Kling turns it into a real moving clip. The kettle fills the glass with chai, steam rises, and the camera slowly moves in. All from text. It cost 30 credits. Today you make it yourself, with every setting explained.

## What you'll learn

- Which menus to pick for text to video in Kling, and why

- How to write the prompt with the formula from Module 3
- What each setting does: mode, duration, ratio and output
- What happens after you press Generate, and why you press it only once
- How to check the result like a director, and save it

## Before you start

- **Account:** your free Kling account from M05-L01.
- **Cost:** this lesson spends credits once. Kling 4.0 Flash, 720p, 5 seconds, 9:16, one output = **30 credits**. In the video, the balance went from 612 to 582.
- **Watermark:** the free plan always adds a small Kling watermark in the corner.

## Follow along

### Step 1 · Open Generate and pick the menus

Open **Generate**, and look at the menus under the prompt box.

1. **What to make:** pick **Video Generation**.
2. **Model:** pick **Kling 4.0 Flash**, the cheap one.
3. **Reference mode:** this menu has two modes.

| Mode | What it uses |
|---|---|
| Omni reference | your text, and any pictures you add |
| Keyframes | a start picture and an end picture |

For text to video, keep **Omni reference**, and add no picture. Only words.

### Step 2 · Write the prompt with the formula

Use the formula from Module 3, part by part, in one paragraph:

| Part | The words |
|---|---|
| Subject + action | A steel kettle pours a long stream of hot masala chai into a small glass |
| Place | on a wooden counter at a roadside chai stall. |
| Detail | Steam rises from the glass. |
| Light | Early morning, soft golden sunlight. |
| Camera | Close-up, slow push in. |
| Style | Realistic, shallow depth of field. |
| End | No text. |

Then paste the full paragraph into the prompt box (it is in "Prompts used" below).

### Step 3 · Open the settings

Open the settings, next to the Generate button.

| Setting | What to pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | 720p | Flash makes 720p only. That is sharp enough for a phone |
| Duration | 5 seconds | It goes from 3 to 20 seconds. Keep it at 5 |
| Ratio | 9:16 | 9:16 is tall, for Reels and Shorts. 16:9 is wide, for YouTube |
| Output | 1 | How many clips at once. 2, 3 and 4 are VIP |

Now the button says **30**.

### Step 4 · Press Generate, once

Press **Generate** one time. **This spends 30 credits.**

What you should see:

- The credits leave your balance right away. In the video, 612 became 582.
- Then your clip waits in a queue. Kling showed about 4 minutes. Free accounts wait longer: that is what **Subscribe for faster generation** means.

Do not press Generate again while you wait. Every press is a new clip, and new credits.

Click path: `Generate > Video Generation > Kling 4.0 Flash > Omni reference > 720p · 5 s · 9:16 · 1 output > Generate`

### Step 5 · Check it like a director

Ask three questions:

| Question | The video's clip |
|---|---|
| Did it follow the prompt? | The kettle pours, the glass fills, and steam rises. Yes |
| Did the camera move? | A slow push in. Yes |
| Any text? | None. Only the small Kling watermark in the corner. The free plan always adds it |

### Step 6 · Save it

Happy with it? Point at the clip, click the **download arrow**, and pick **MP4**.

## Prompts used

```text
A steel kettle pours a long stream of hot masala chai into a small glass on a wooden counter at a roadside chai stall. Steam rises from the glass. Early morning, soft golden sunlight. Close-up, slow push in. Realistic, shallow depth of field. No text.
```

## Common mistakes

1. **Too many actions.** 5 seconds fits one simple action. Filling one glass, yes. Filling it, drinking it, and walking away, no.
2. **The wrong ratio.** A 16:9 clip will not fill a phone screen (you get black bars). Pick 9:16 before you generate.
3. **Unclear words,** like "a nice chai video". Then Kling guesses. Describe what the camera sees: "a steel kettle pours chai into a small glass".

The fix: one action, 9:16, and clear words.
