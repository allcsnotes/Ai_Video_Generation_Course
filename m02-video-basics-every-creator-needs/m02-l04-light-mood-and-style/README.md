# M02-L04 · Light, Mood and Style

> Video: Module 2, Lesson 4. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Here is our chai cup in warm morning light. Now the same cup at night, under a cold blue light. Same cup, same camera, but the light changed the whole feeling.

In a prompt, what changes the mood more: the subject, or the light? The light. Light, mood and style are often what make a clip look professional, or cheap.

## What you'll learn

- Five light words and the feeling each one gives
- Where the light comes from: front, side and back
- Mood words and the colours that carry mood
- Style words: cinematic, documentary and cartoon looks
- How to put shot, subject, camera, light, mood and style in one prompt
- Three light and style mistakes

## Key ideas

### Light words do most of the work

| Light | What it is | Feels |
|---|---|---|
| Golden hour | Warm, soft light just after sunrise or before sunset | Warm and happy |
| Blue hour | Cool light just after sunset | Calm, a little sad |
| Harsh midday | Strong sun, hard shadows | Hot and real |
| Soft and cloudy | Almost no shadows | Kind to faces |
| Neon at night | Coloured city lights | Modern, exciting, a bit mysterious |

### Where the light comes from

| Direction | Effect |
|---|---|
| Front light | Shows everything clearly, but can look flat |
| Side light | One side bright, the other dark: adds drama |
| Backlight | Comes from behind the subject: a glowing edge, so steam and hair look magical |

### Mood words

Mood words tell the AI how the scene should feel: cozy, calm, scary, dreamy, energetic. One mood word can change the colours, the light and even the motion.

Colours carry mood too:

| Colours | Feel |
|---|---|
| Warm oranges | Friendly |
| Cool blues | Quiet |
| Strong contrast | Dramatic |

### Style: the look of the whole video

| Style | Looks like |
|---|---|
| Cinematic | A movie: a wide frame, rich colours, a soft background |
| Documentary | Real and natural, as if someone filmed it on the spot |
| Anime style, 3D animation, claymation | A cartoon. You will make these yourself later in the course |

Pick one style per video, and keep it the same in every shot.

### All of it in one prompt

Shot size, subject, camera move (from the last lesson), then the light, the mood and the style.

| # | Part | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shot size | close-up |
| 2 | Subject | a cup of chai |
| 3 | Camera | slow push in |
| 4 | Light | golden backlight |
| 5 | Mood | cozy |
| 6 | Style | cinematic |

```text
close-up of a clay cup of chai, steam rising, slow push in, golden hour backlight, cozy mood, cinematic
```

## Worked example

The two chai cups from the opening:

| Version | Light | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm morning light | Warm, friendly |
| 2 | Night, cold blue light | Quiet, a little sad |

Only the light words changed. Backlight is the trick in the final prompt: light from behind makes the steam glow, which is exactly what a chai shot needs.

## Common mistakes

- **No light words.** The AI gives flat, boring light. Always name the light.
- **Mixing styles in one prompt,** like anime and realistic. Pick one.
- **Too many mood words.** Cozy, scary and happy at once confuses the AI. Use one or two.

Safe default: one light, one or two moods, one style.

2. Rewrite the final chai prompt for a calm evening version: change only the light and the mood.

## Answers
