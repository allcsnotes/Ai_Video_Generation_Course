# M13-L04 · Build 8: Your Own Project

> Video: Module 13, Build 8. Watch it first, then use this page to revise and practise.

Build 8 is **your own project**. This time you pick the idea, choose the tool with the **decision chart** (Module 8), and take it all the way to the upload. The video's example: **a rainy evening at Chai Corner**, a 15-second YouTube Short with no talking, only rain and chai.

## What you'll build

- A 15-second vertical Short (9:16, 1080 × 1920) from one idea of your own
- Three Kling clips, chosen with the decision chart
- A real rain recording as the only sound, with fades
- A title, a soft ending, and an upload to YouTube as **Private**, with **AI use = Yes**

## Before you start

- The **decision chart** from Module 8 ([download it](../../downloads/kling-vs-omni-decision-chart.md)).
- **Kling** account with credits (Module 5).
- **Pixabay** for a free sound (Module 10).
- **Shotcut** installed (free).
- A **YouTube** account, and access to **YouTube Studio**.
- **Credits:** each clip costs **30 credits**. Three clips is **90 credits**, so keep **30 more for one fix**: **120 credits** in all. The video did need that fix. Prices and plans change: check the current price before you buy.
- Fill in the [build plan template](../../downloads/build-plan-template.md) before you spend a credit.

## Plan

### One idea, in one sentence

> A rainy evening at a chai stall, warm and cosy.

### Ask the decision chart questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 · Does someone speak? | No |
| 2 · Must a person hold a product? | No |
| 3 · Is it a close-up of food, with no people? | Yes |

So the chart says **Kling 4.0 Flash**, at **720p** and **5 seconds**. It makes rich, real-looking detail, and we add the sound ourselves.

### Plan the clips and the credits

All clips in **9:16**, for a Short.

## Build it step by step

### Step 1 · Make the clips in Kling

1. In Kling, choose **Video Generation** and **Kling 4.0 Flash**. Then set **720p**, **5 s**, and **9:16**.
2. Clip 1: a close-up of heavy rain dripping from a tin roof at dusk, with a warm yellow bulb glowing behind it.
3. Every prompt ends the same way: `No people. No text.` Then click **Generate**.
4. Clip 2 shows the chai being poured. But the **first try came out pale**, like milky tea, not the dark brown we wanted.
5. So we **fixed the prompt**: "hot milky masala chai" became "hot brown masala chai, the colour of caramel". The new clip looks right. That fix cost **30 more credits**.
6. Clip 3: a small glass of chai on a wooden counter, steam curling up, and rain on the window behind it.
7. **Watch every clip all the way through.** Then download them, and number the names: `1 rain`, `2 pour`, `3 glass`.

### Step 2 · The sound: real rain

In this video, the rain is the star, so we use a real rain recording.

1. On **Pixabay**, open **Sound Effects**, and search for **rain on roof**. Play a few, and pick one that sounds soft and steady.
2. Check that it is free to use under the **Pixabay Content License**. Then click **Free download**, and keep a note of the name and the link.

### Step 3 · Build the edit in Shotcut

1. New project called **Rainy Evening**. For **Video mode**, open **Social Media**, and choose **Vertical HD 30 fps**.
2. Open the three clips and the rain sound together. Then double-click each clip in order, and press **A**. Three clips, 15 seconds.
3. Click the **speaker icon on V1** to mute the clips. The rain recording does all the sound.
4. Add an audio track. Open the rain sound, go to **15 seconds**, and press **O** to mark the end. Then select **A1**, and press **A**.
5. Select the rain sound on the audio track, and add two filters: **Fade In Audio** for **1 second**, and **Fade Out Audio** for **2 seconds**.
6. On clip 1, add **Text: Simple**, and type the title on two lines: `Rainy evening` / `at Chai Corner`.
7. Set **Position** to **90, 1300** and **Size** to **900 × 300**, with **Middle**. The title sits low, where it reads well on a phone.
8. On clip 3, add **Fade Out Video** for **1 second**, so the video ends softly.
9. Export with the **YouTube** preset. The Short is **15 seconds** long, **1080 × 1920**.

### Step 4 · Upload it, privately

1. In **YouTube Studio**, click the **upload** button, and select your video file.
2. Give it a clear title: **Rainy evening at Chai Corner**. In the description, say what it shows, and that AI clips were used.
3. Under **Audience**, choose **No, it's not made for kids**.
4. Click **Show more**, and find **AI use**. This scene looks real, but it never happened, so choose **Yes**. YouTube adds a label.
5. Click **Next** through **Video elements** and **Checks**. In the video, the copyright check found no issues.
6. For your first upload, choose **Private**, and click **Save**. Only you can see it, so you can check it on your phone first.

The video uploaded its Short as **Private**, with AI use set to **Yes**.

## Prompts used

Settings for every clip: `Video Generation · Kling 4.0 Flash · 720p · 5 s · 9:16` (30 credits each).

Clip 1, rain:

```text
Clip 1. Close-up of heavy rain dripping from the edge of a tin roof at a small roadside chai stall at dusk. A warm yellow bulb glows behind the rain. Slow camera push in. Cinematic, realistic, shallow depth of field. No people. No text.
```

Clip 2, first try (came out pale, like milky tea):

```text
Clip 2. Extreme close-up of hot milky masala chai being poured from a steel kettle into a small glass on a wooden counter. Steam rises. Rain falls softly in the blurred background, warm bulb light. Slow motion. Cinematic, realistic. No people. No text.
```

Clip 2, fixed (30 more credits):

```text
Clip 2. Extreme close-up of hot brown masala chai, the colour of caramel, being poured from a steel kettle into a small glass on a wooden counter. The glass fills with the same brown chai. Steam rises. Rain falls softly in the blurred background, warm bulb light. Slow motion. Cinematic, realistic. No people. No text.
```

Clip 3, the glass by the window:

```text
Clip 3. Close-up of a small glass of masala chai on a wooden counter, steam curling up. Behind it, rain streaks down and warm street lights glow out of focus. Gentle slow camera push in. Cinematic, realistic, cosy. No people. No text.
```

Pixabay search: `Sound Effects > rain on roof`.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting without one clear idea.** Write it in one sentence first.
- **Picking a tool by habit.** Ask the decision chart questions.
- **No credits left for a fix.** Keep a spare 30 for one remake.
- **Accepting a clip that looks wrong.** The pale chai was fixed by changing the prompt, not by hoping.
- **Using a sound you are not allowed to use.** Check the licence, and keep the name and link.
- **Saying No to AI use.** The scene looks real but never happened: choose Yes.
- **Going Public on the first upload.** Upload as Private and check it first.

## Your task

Make your own 15-second Short about something you love, and upload it as Private.

What good looks like: a filled-in build plan (idea, tool, clips, credits, sound, edit, upload), the tool chosen by the decision chart, every clip watched and fixed where needed, real or licensed sound, a clean Shotcut export, and a Private upload with AI use = Yes.

## Recap

1. Start with one idea, in one sentence.
2. Use the decision chart to pick the tool, then plan the clips and the credits.
3. Watch every clip, and fix the prompt when one looks wrong.
4. Add real sound, edit in Shotcut, and export with the YouTube preset.
5. Upload as Private, say Yes to AI use, and check it before anyone else sees it.
