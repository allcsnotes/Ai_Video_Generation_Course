# AI Video Creation Course: Resources

Companion files for the **AI Video Creation** video course: one folder per video (70 in all: 62 lessons and 8 builds), each with a step-by-step README you can read after watching, plus templates, checklists and a prompt library you can download and use for your own videos.

The course follows one small example business, **Chai Corner** (a roadside chai stall), and its AI-made character **Meera**, from the first prompt to finished Reels, ads, a short film and a music video. Meera and every Chai Corner scene are made with AI: no real person is shown.

## How to use this repo

1. Watch the video first.
2. Open the folder with the same lesson ID (for example `M05-L02`) and read its README.
3. Copy the prompts, follow the numbered steps in your own accounts, and do the "Try it yourself" tasks.
4. Copy the templates from `downloads/` and use them for your own projects and clients.

Lesson READMEs come in three kinds:

| Kind | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| Concept lessons | Key ideas, Worked example, Common mistakes, Try it yourself (with answers) |
| Tool walkthroughs (Claude, ChatGPT, Kling, Google Flow, CapCut, Shotcut, YouTube Studio) | Before you start, Follow along (exact click paths), Prompts used, Common mistakes |
| Builds (Build 1 to 8) | What you'll build, Plan, Build it step by step, Prompts used, Check your work |

## What you need

- A laptop and an internet connection.
- Free accounts: Claude, ChatGPT, Kling, CapCut desktop or Shotcut. Everything except Omni in Google Flow can be learned on free plans.
- Optional: Google AI Plus for Omni 1.2 Flash in Google Flow (the course used ₹399 a month; check the current price before you buy).
- Free plans may add a watermark and may not allow commercial use. Upgrade only when a paying client needs it, and read each tool's current terms.

## Safety and honesty

- Never fake a real person. Get written consent before you use anyone's face or voice.
- Label realistic AI content when you upload it (for example YouTube's "AI use" setting).
- Never crop or blur out a tool's watermark: upgrade to a plan that allows clean exports instead.
- Use music you have a licence for, and keep the proof.
- Honest marketing only: no fake reviews, fake counts or fake scarcity.
